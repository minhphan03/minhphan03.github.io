One of the defining albums of this decade for me so far must be RENAISSANCE by Beyoncé, a powerful, feministic, culturally classic, and deterministic statement from the queen B herself. In RENAISSANCE, Beyonce reinstates what it means for Black (and queer) music to be: fun, catchy, profound, sneaky, sensual, powerful, and dynamic. We see the hundred of years’ worth of musical elements wrapped in an album of sixteen songs, crafted perfectly to the core from the minds of hundreds of collaborators under queen B’s grand vision. A concept album filled to the brim to satisfy the fantasy of millions of music lovers around the world, RENAISSANCE is also a testament to and an anthem of the beautiful, prosperous, and everlasting human culture. In the wise words of herself, “It’s so good.”
