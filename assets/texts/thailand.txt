I visited Thailand with my family over ten years ago, and ten years later, on a solo trip, I watch it begin again. I couldn't recall anything on my first time visiting it, so this is a great opportunity for me to rediscover this country. This is my first true solo trip, and I learned a lot from it: budgeting, time management, and trip planning when incident happens. I believe everyone should embrace solo traveling at least once in their lifetime. It is a lesson of growth, of exploring the human nature, and opening yourself to the endless possibilities of the world. I'm so grateful for my decision to carry out this trip, because it turned out to be the catalyst I needed to kickstart this new year.
