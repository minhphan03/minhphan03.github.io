I went to San Francisco during Spring break 2023 with one of my friends (she actually went to look at some apartments for her twin sister to stay during summer for her internship at Salesforce), but we did not really travel together. I stayed in downtown, while her family stayed in the southern Bay Area. Therefore, I got a lot of free time to travel on my own, and it was an absolute blast: I walked around the Baker Beach at 10p where there was no one but only the sound of ocean waves undulating around me. I walked to the Golden Gate bridge; I bought some donuts; I ate at Chinatown. You should know it was the first time I ever did solo travel, so I haven't unlocked my full potential. Hopefully when I earn more $$, I can afford those kinds of trips again.
