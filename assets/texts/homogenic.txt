I don't think I am an experimental music enjoyer, but if there is one experimental project that I absolutely adore, it's Björk's third full-length LP, Homogenic. It is wild, it is critically acclaim, and it is consummately ahead of its time. Unsurprisingly, it's also very Björk, which I do think is a perfect adjective to describe it. No one but herself can be this forward thinking and record these songs this type of way 25 years ago.
