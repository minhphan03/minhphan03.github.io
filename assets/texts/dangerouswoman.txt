When I first discovered Ariana Grande, she was more or less another potential popstar in the pop market: her voice powerful, her songs catchy, and her image calculated. Dangerous Woman, her very first album that I listened to in full, was exactly just that: commercial, industrial, the type of album that you would expect from someone who wants to conquer pop. A more mature approach to market her image, Grande’s third studio album and era did its thing to me. From the title track to “Thinking Bout You,” she effortlessly delivered a buffet of fantastic bangers and sexy tunes that further cemented her legacy. To this day, Dangerous Woman is still my favorite album of hers, next to Thank U, Next, which is also a fan-favorite that you should check out!
