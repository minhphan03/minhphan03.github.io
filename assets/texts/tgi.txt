Halsey’s newest album was made during the most difficult years of her life: a divorce, a battle with Lupus (alluded to by the album title), and handling being a single mother for the first time. It was no wonder why the album’s subjects were pure heavy, in which she confessed, “I wake up every day in some new kind of suffering / I’ve never known a day of peace” (“Only Girl Living in LA”). Her lyricism was poignant and crisp, her emotions rich and heart-wrenching yet hopeful at times, and the songs ranged from the softest, dreamiest lullabies to the furious outpourings of anger and steam. The album does not only showcase her capabilities as one of the most versatile artists in the current climate, but also her bravery to not shy away from baring the bruised, raw human beneath the mask shaped from that exact chameleonic artistry.
