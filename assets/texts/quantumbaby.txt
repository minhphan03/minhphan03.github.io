I mentioned this album a little bit in my review of “Thirsty” in my favorite 2024 songs post, and that review basically summed up why I enjoy this album immensely. It’s sultry, dirty (positively), containing these all-nocturnal vibes that you just want to put it on at night for a slow dance in the dark, and full of sonic callbacks from her early works. Masterful usage of vocal effects, basses, synths, and electronica show off Tinashe’s incredible songwriting skills and her collaborators’ production efforts. The whole project is uniquely Tinashe R&B that no one else can replicate. Tell me who can create something like “Thirsty” or “I Can Get You Alone?” with a switch-up at the end like that? Exactly.
