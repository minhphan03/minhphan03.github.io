New York was fun: I loved it so so much, and I can't wait to go back there again: full of life, full of people, and nothing short of energy. It was everything I thought and more. I stayed there for ten days and it felt like two, simply because there were so many places I wanted to explore but our 1-hour subway trip from the Bronx simply took too much time out of our expeditions. From Washington Park to Statue of Liberty, from Manhattan to Brooklyn, from West Village to Harlem, everything was so grand and full of culture. With that being said, I would never dream of living there, as everything's damn costly (it's like saying water is wet!!)
