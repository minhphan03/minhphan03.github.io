E•MO•TION (yes, fully capitalized with the bullets, please) is legitimately one of my top five favorite albums ever. There’s no words to describe the feeling I had the first time I listened to this album five years ago. Every song on this album still feels so fresh despite being released ten years ago. “Run Away With Me” is still had me pumping my adrenaline every time it comes on, and “Your Type” is the most underrated song ever released (despite being made a single). You just cannot find fault in any single song in this collection of the original twelve songs (and three deluxe songs, two bonus Japanese songs, one standalone single, and four new original songs for its ten year anniversary edition release, which I’m featuring on here). I can put any song in this album any time at any day and having a field day with it. An example is “Lost in Devotion,” which I just put on repeat for two days straight this week. It’s just that insanely good, and as much as I love her, I don’t think she has come close to top it ever, with The Loneliest Time being a close runner-up.
