i just happen to believe that everyone has that music album that they think is an instant classic, inside and out, and to me it’s Lana Del Rey’s fifth major-label studio album, Norman Fucking Rockwell!, a stunning, bright, gorgeous, invigorating, effortless feast of music from one of the America’s most talented songwriters of our generation. It is that kind of music that lives up to its title — I had to blurbed out the f-word after finishing it because it was just simply too good. “Venice Bitch,” her longest song to date, is carthatic and complex, a testament to her capability to break out of the jaded construction of typical pop songs; “Cinnamon Girl” is insanely light, breezy and depressing; and “Norman Fucking Rockwell” reminisces the old glory Lana Del Rey from her “Born to Die” era, reckless, cold and oh so old Americana that defined the signature Tumblr era in mid 2010s.
