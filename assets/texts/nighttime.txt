If you want an album that personifies the brightest nights, the gloriest limelights, the exquisite alcoholic feelings, but also the hollow aftertastes of crazy parties and dead silence of the self, whose reputation is attached to those LA vibes everyone has heard of, behind the closed doors, listen to Sky's debut album. It is a once-in-a-million, one that she still resonates until now (and hesitates to release her next one because of how iconic it is!)
