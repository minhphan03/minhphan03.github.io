This album is the epitome of hidden gem, and an underdog in the copious amount of releases this year. It seems like everyone and their mothers are releasing albums, for real, and I’m not kidding. Being a casual listener to Ms. Cabello, I was intrigued by the lead single of the album when she first teased it on social media because it sounded so… dissonant. You can say I grew used to it, but the more I listened to it, the more I liked it. Call it Stockholm syndrome or whatever, I don’t care. And so the rest of C, XOXO came out and I felt in love with the musical direction she ventured this time. A complete rebrand from her first three albums, C, XOXO is chaotic, tumultuous, dramatic, yet resigned and peaceful at times. It conveyed the emotions of someone who’s established in the music industry long enough to feel like they need to experiment more and have enough courage and confidence they can make it. And she did what she set out to do. “June Gloom” has a cathartic outro that became an instant earworm; “Dade County Dreaming” feat the girls from City Girls were nothing short of amazing and surprising (that piano break on top of the rap bars are crazy); “HE KNOWS” is a club banger I fully embrace from Ms. Cabello; and how can I forget “GODSPEED” — so haunting, so liberating, and a perfect reminder of those elements that her fans have fallen in love for, a testament of her songwriting skills.
