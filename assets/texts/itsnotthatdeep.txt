With three days left before the It’s Not That Deep concert in DC, I finally sit down and listened to her newest project beyond “Fast,” its first single. Not to cause any misinterpretation, the song was excellent; however, I was never a casual fan of Demi, and knowing my listening habit, I would rather stick with artists I knew I would like. “Fast” was an exception, because it went viral in the pop music forums I frequent to, but  “Here All Night,” the second single, was not as instantly catchy as the other. I instantly caught on some songs in album (the phenomenal “Kiss” and “Joshua Tree”) after the first listen, but it was not until the actual concert for me to appreciate, belatedly, this underrated dance record, including its recent eight tracks addition on the deluxe version (shout out to “After Hours” and “Low Rise Jeans,” so immaculate and hypnotizing).
