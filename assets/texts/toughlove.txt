Opening the new year with one of the old albums that I only recently discovered and listened in full (recently being almost a year and a half ago). I was first introducted and started listening to Jessie Ware when her song “Spotlight” became a sensation, then fell in love with What’s Your Pleasure? and advanced to check out her discovery. Tough Love was definitely my favorite of hers, with all songs (heavy on the word “all”) being enjoyable. “Cruel” is a hidden gem; “Say You Love Me” 100% deserves it flowers; “Sweetest Song” was dripping with sensuality; and “All On You” was probably the best song I’ve ever heard in fall 2024.
