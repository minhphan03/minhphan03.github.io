I’ve been waiting for a feel-good album to sweep me away from the ordinances life imposes on for a long long time, and Higher Than Heaven was the savior. I knew from intuition, just by looking at the album and the title, that it would not let me down and fortunately it did not. Temptation at its best, I checked out and instantly fell in love with the pre-singles “Let It Die” and “Easy Lover.” I was so excited, because I’ve been waiting for that IT factor, and it arrived and it blew my mind. I finished the album by the end of the release night, feeling like a better man, that it would be a great companion of my summer days ahead. You could say I’ve got a heartbeat just for it, at least in the moment.
