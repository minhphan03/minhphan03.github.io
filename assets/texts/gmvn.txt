Phùng Khánh Linh was an underdog in the Viet music scene in which it took her almost a decade from her debut hit “Hôm Nay Tôi Buồn” to outgrow, rise up, and gain considerable popularity among her fellows and from V-Pop fans. During these years, with admirable persistence and perseverance, as well as her talent as a songwriter, Linh wrote and released three full-length albums, all (especially GIỮA MỘT VẠN NGƯỜI, her latest) received praises for her artistry, experimentation, and the quality standards that she put herself through in constant renewal of her images. It eventually all paid off handsomely. With the hit single “ANH LÀ THẰNG TỒI” which went viral on social media as she performed the song throughout countless festivals, and the powerful, enchanting, technically impressive single “EM ĐAU” (feat Thành Luke), one by one masterpiece after another, she shows herself to be one of the most promising, shining artists in the current music industry and artistic landscape. As a long-term fan, I can’t wait to witness the new heights of success that I’m sure she will reach in the future.
