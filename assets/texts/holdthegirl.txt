In Hold the Girl, Rina Sawayama told her stories of abuse, shame, freedom, and growth as a coping mechanism (very artist of her, truly) towards the old skeletons in her closet. In “Phantom,” she shared her issues of people pleasing and neglecting her own self; “Holy (’Til You Let Me Go)” is a powerful eurodance-fused track about religion trauma; “Minor Feelings” is directed towards racial micro-aggressions. However, it does not mean that the whole album is a socio-political tirade: “This Hell” is Sawayama with her most sexual confidence and freedom, while “Flavor of the Month” (a bonus track) is nothing short of sexiness oozing.
