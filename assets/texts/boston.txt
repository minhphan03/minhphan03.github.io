I drove to Boston on a road trip with one of my friends for the holidays, and let me tell you, it was freaking freezing. It was so cold that we decided we would never come back here, like ever again, during the cold season, and, on top of the unfortunate weather that we just never imagined how suffering we would be, I just happened to forget my camera as well. So that was bad, bad. The not-so-bad thing was, I recently bought a new phone, so this trip really forced me to make the most out of mobile photography… I can at least say that the photos turned out to be not so terrible (and I could have taken more if I had that camera with me instead of fidgeting for the phone every time I wanted to capture something by removing my gloves and exposing my fragile hands to the freezing breezes of Massachusetts). You can also note that I took way more vertical photos this time (there’s a dedicated album for it on my Flickr), which can be attributed to the situation.
