Troye Sivan is the only male artist I actively listen to, and part of the reason why his music resonates with me deeply probably comes from the fact that he's a proud gay man. His music is sensual, addictive, and while Blue Neighborhood is not really my jam, Bloom, In A Dream, and Something To Give Each Other are total bangers of projects. "In My Room" (feat. Guitarricadelafuente) was chill, somber, and groovy, and so are "What's The Time Where You Are?" and "Rush" (I did not like the music video of the latter, but the song itself was so insanely ass-shaking-worthy). "How to Stay with You" was on repeat for a couple of weeks straight, and don't get me started on "One Of Your Girls" and "Can't Go Back, Baby"... Five years of waiting for a ten-track album may sound frustrating, but if that said album has absolutely no filler, I guess it's worth it.
