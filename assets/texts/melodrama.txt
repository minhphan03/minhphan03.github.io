Being a teenager is hard, but ending the teenager phase is harder: you are forced to strip off your innocence and brace yourself to the adult world, and in the meantime you are going to embarrass yourself and make a lot of mistakes. Lorde knows this more than anyone: catapulted to stardom at a very young age, she caught herself in multitudes of emotions, not excluding falling in and out of love, dealing with liabilities and watching the world losing its magic. "Melodrama" is a masterpiece, a musical that resonates with almost any teenager, and a recollection of what a melodramatic love looks like before it fizzles out.
