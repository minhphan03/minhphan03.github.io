I thought this EP’s production is lovely and aging well personally. The four songs are short but have decent replay value. I do not understand Korean and not planning to learn but again, the highlight here is the production and JISOO’s ear for great jamming production showed pretty clear here. This is my favorite solo debut of all the BLACKPINK members in terms of overall enjoyment. The other girls may have more songs I enjoy but for the “all bangers no killer” criterion, this one takes the cake.
