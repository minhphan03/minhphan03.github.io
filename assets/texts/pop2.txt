I was only a casual Charli XCX’s listener until I listened to her second studio mixtape, POP2, last year, and only ten tracks were needed to turn me into an "Angel" (a fan of Charli XCX). Quirky, futuristic, and totally boundless, POP2 is what defined and singled Charli out of her pop peers at the time. Mostly produced by AG Cook of PC Music and Sophie, the popular pioneers in hyperpop genre, these tracks were bombastic and unlike anything I’ve ever heard before. It also defined and set the signature for Charli’s future releases, notably in her next two albums "Charli" & "How I’m Feeling Now."
