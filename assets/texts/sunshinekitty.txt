I was a casual listener to Tove Lo until I went to her concert last March, and it blew me away how fiery and solid her discography has been so far. Lately I've been playing Sunshine Kitty on loop, and it's honestly baffling how I dismissed it a few years ago and doubted my friend when he said it was his favorite album of hers. Now that I am able to immerse myself fully into this body of work, I honestly have to give her the crown. "Really don't like u" with Kylie Minogue was a bop, "Mateo" is gorgeous, "Jacques" with Jax Jones is a dance slay, and "I'm Coming" is definitely one of my favorite song covers ever ever ever ever!
