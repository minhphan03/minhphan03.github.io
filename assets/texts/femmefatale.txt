Britney Spears’ 7th studio album, Femme Fatale, despite produced during her conservatorship (and I know we should not glorify it because we all know it was a hard time for the queen), was a masterclass in pop bangers, all being tasteful, clubby, and euphoric in all the right ways. To commemorate “Selfish,” a bonus track, unknown, no-promotion song hitting #1 on US iTunes 13 years after its release, I introduce you to this album as a die-hard fan of Britney. Packed with hits, from “Hold It Against Me” to “Criminal,” Femme Fatale made waves in the pop scene during the early 10s and was regarded as a staple and signature of what it is now called the early 2010’s nostalgia. What a simpler time back then!
