I went to Singapore for the first time with my family a very long while ago, but this is the first time I visited the country by myself. It was somewhat familiar yet new through the eyes of an eighteen-year-old. I only stayed there for one night, but I still had the time to walk through the streets to the sea lion statue and bought a Starbucks cup on the way there. It (the sea lion) seemed bigger than I thought. I did not see the appeal of Marina Bay Sands, though.
