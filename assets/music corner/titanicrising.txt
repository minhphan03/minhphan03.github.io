There’s something about Weyes Blood (Natalie Mering) that wholly intrigues me: her deep, nostalgic voice that reeks class and age. I think that is part of the reason why Andromeda, the first and only song I had of her in my library for a long time, was put on loop for many a time. Upon releasing her new album in 2022, “And In The Darkness, Hearts Aglow,” I decided to revisit “Titanic Rising” again and discovered a lot of hidden gems in this album. When you put on “Mirror Forever” and “Movies," there's a chance that your life will never be the same again. The instrumentals are unmatched, and there’s a reason why the album ranked so high on the year-end lists of many reputable music newspapers.
