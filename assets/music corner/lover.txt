Many fans overlook “Lover” as Taylor Swift’s weakest pop album because choosing “ME!” as her first single was definitely… a choice. It did not completely shatter her reputation or anything like that, but it made her a laughing stock for quite some time. However, the album itself is my guilty pleasure, and as much as I would prefer Taylor to not release that song, it’s a fun, lighthearted bubblegum pop that many may need to cheer them up in the darkest times, probably a year later when the world shut down as a result of the pandemic. “London Boy,” “Cornelia Street,” and “Cruel Summer” are just some of the hidden gems from the album that deserve their newfound surge of exposure (and love from the general public) as our Americana Queen kicked off her long-awaited “The Eras Tour.”
