Seasonal depression is quite literally depressing, so I guess it’s time to pump the heat up with some dancing fun pop songs. Dirt Femme, the 5th studio album and the first as an independent artist of Tove Lo, is wild, sexy, contemplative, and everything in between. It is lighthearted yet down-to-earth, flirty yet sultry, and her most interest one yet. I have had “I’m to Blame” and “Grapefruit” on repeat recently, and honestly you cannot go wrong with any song in this album. I go bonkers wishing this album would get more recognition, but I guess another selfish part in me is elated that this is part of an undiscovered culture, where authenticity stays intact as artists do not feel the need to chase after musical fads.
