Artistically, Carly Rae Jepsen is a gem in the pop genre scene: consistent birth of high-quality pop songs, and exceptional delivery of experimentation and familiarity, all at the same time. The Loneliest Time is the attestation to her mastery of the craft, from the banger of the first track "Surrender My Heart" to the heartfelt ballad "Go Find Yourself or Whatever", lose yourself in the unique "music-scape" from one of my favorite artists ever. Kudos, Carly!
