oh i definitely know better that pop music has already been so hyper-sexualized (britney, madonna, prince, etc.) and gay men singing about sex openly and shamelessly is not a new thing (freddie mercury and george michael are just a few), but it’s still so wild to me how troye did it. i guess as a teen growing up listening to his music (addicted to at one point), i had this very set image about who he was as what people called a “cupcake”: sweet, innocent, and affectionate from the days of "blue neighborhood." i didn’t think much about how could pull off such a slutty image (no slutshaming here, and honestly he did hint a little bit of that side of him in the music video for angel baby or even stud) hence this total meltdown. in a way, it likens britney’s transformation to her britney era from oops..., and now i can truly empathize with people who has witnessed that: it’s staggering, shocking, and for many, disappointing. i felt the exact same thing. i felt lost, and ashamed of how this parasocial relationship truly affected me. 

rush is not a bad song per se: it got crowned best new music from pitchfork, and its 90’s house club elements were pretty cool for a song about sexual energy, but i just could not vibe with it like the old songs on his previous albums. the music video may play a big role in it, but the song itself was representative enough. maybe he wanted to do it, or he had to reinvent himself, but i don’t think i can vibe with this new thing. i’m sorry troye, but i’ll see if “something to give each other” is a project i’m willing to engage. im not asking him to change his image for people like me or include more representation in this new video (white twinks occupied like 90% of people in that video and i felt like throwing up), because we are all sexual beings, but i just need to get this off my chest. im happy for him that he could pull this off, but it’s no longer something i can relate to in the slightest. it's not like his old image was that relatable: i never fell in love with anyone to sing "animal" or "lucky strikes" in earnest. but i know from the deepest corner of my heart i am that type of person. now, to this new era, i can never find myself vibing to any of what he has given so far: i’m not a clubby person. i don’t do hookups or sexualize myself to the point that it has to revolve around my ideals. poppers is a no-no. i may sound conservative or even gay-cel, but but but but but i need time to open up to this image. maybe one day, i hope, because as a fellow gay man we need to support each other, not tear them down. i’d still support your old projects, and you’re always loved.

so many words for so little a first world problem. but you know it’s hard to feel unrepresented and unrelated to the person you look up to for a long time, for the first time in your life. it was definitely something to experience. 

minh xoxo 14.7.23
