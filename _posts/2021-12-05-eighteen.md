So today is the last day of my seventeenth year on Earth. It’s a mixed feeling if I have to be honest with myself. I want to write down something if I am to reread this post someday and remember how crazy I have been dreaming about the future. So what should I write now? That time is just a social construct and if I kept believing that I’m still 17 and faked my papers I would stay underage for one more year? Sounds about righttttt.

I reread my blog post on my 17th birthday and I’m surprised it still resonates so well with my mindset today. Guess a year later haven’t changed me that much, because I, according to that post, had been forced to mature and it bore a lot of consequences on my self-confidence and lack of security. I’m struggling still. I’m making so much fuss I’m tired to deny. I also said age is not important, but why am I here again about to write another blog post talking about how I’m officially one year older?

Eighteen is a milestone because as soon as you turn eighteen, you are not considered “underage” anymore (except for drinking age). You suddenly have to carry more responsibilities on your hands (financial affairs, education choices) as you’re suddenly have the access to almost every business. Bank accounts, career choices, etc. are only a few. They expose you to unseen risks but unsurmountable lessons to learn as part of adulting. So I guess I understand why a lot of people want to stay young forever, because as they age, they have to look at life with a more experienced and sensible perspective, which can be painful and horrendous at times. They lose their enthusiasm to explore their best self in trade of a safer approach to adulthood. I myself have been there, and I have to admit that there’s not a few times I just want to crawl back into my bed and pretend the world outside does not exist. But it still exists and I am here today to celebrate my own birthday, a social construct that will die as soon as I disappear on Earth.

A birthday can be a notch on somebody’s belt. In my case, it can be when I gather enough reasons why living on this land is not a struggle every day. For all I know, it’s a perfect reminder that I have managed to survive one more year on this Earth. At least I know that is a universal truth. Or one more day, as I wake up and see that the sun still rises tomorrow; the world moves on, completely ignorant of how special tomorrow is for me (and many others). I cannot freeze time and make these days last forever, but I know that if I write this down, at least I will relive this moment over and over again.

Here’s my simple whimsy hopes for the next year: make more friends, believe more in myself, get a better job, and have a fantastic summer in Vietnam (at least got some surgery running please).

Well, here is a song for 18… “Class of 2013” perfectly encapsulates my emotions. Clear consciousness of dependency and desperation of quitting off as a burden to my family. Mitski did it so effortlessly I’m surprised.
