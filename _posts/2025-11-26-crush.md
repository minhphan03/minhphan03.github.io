I mindlessly yet frantically typed out these strands of nonsense as I got bored in my business communications class which I came in at eight in the morning this Monday, not realizing that it was held virtually. There I saw him, the guy. One of the most devastating creatures I have ever had the luck to talk to, and unbeknownst to his charming demeanor, every time we talked he lit my existence on fire. Exactly what I need for a Monday morning. It’s funny because before this I had never obsessed over him that much; if anything, he was just a cute guy sitting at the back of the room with his friends in a class I only begrudgingly came in for the attendance check. Isn’t it so heartachingly, so heartbreaking, how life can be so insane that you have to be born on this planet daydreaming about someone that you can never have, toward someone who you are trying to act so cool and nonchalant outwardly but your heart has already done three thousand five hundred and seventy-three backflips since we have just mutually acknowledged our existence? Then, I ask myself what the fuck am I trying to do with my very little life, willingly bulldozing my heart like this over someone I have barely talked to for weeks. If only he were mean to me (no, I do not have a humiliation kink) and burst the dreamy bubble right from the start. Somehow it would make me feel better. In the words of Taylor Swift, “Could it be enough to just float in your orbit?” I also dreamed, a pathetic dream, to get his attention somehow… maybe a nod, a wave whenever we cross paths on campus that indicates that my existence does register in his mind, that perhaps he would not erase me from his mind after we part our ways. It’s just a humiliation ritual right there, and the sooner we finish this project and never see each other again, the sooner I can just close this chapter for good, put a stop to this panic attack, and be glad that I did not act like a fucking fool. I just can’t do this anymore; please just let me get away and hide away from the rest of the world.

See, this is the biggest reason why I have always tried, for the sake of my life, hiding away from handsome men because I know I can never recover from it. Like those romance books, I would dream about the white picket fence and little kids and what being somebody’s husband would feel like. Pure insanity, I know; I just can’t help where my mind wanders to. I remember around five years ago I had the biggest crush on this guy in my English composition class named Hayden back in Highline, and then I just got so batshit crazy when he accepted my Instagram follow request that I could literally jump from the top of a building, and then tried to not off myself when he unfollowed me five months later when I finished that course, because why the fuck would he dare to light my hopes and follow me to begin with, and why the fuck would I dare to dream about getting to know him on a more personal level? I did not even know if he was gay. Chances were that he was straight as fuck. Just like this guy. He was a wrestler, for fuck’s sake, but that did not even prove anything if I was being honest. He could be a gay wrestler. They must exist, right? He could wreck me anytime, just like this guy and his gold chain dangling in front of my mouth, and I would bark like a lap dog. In hindsight, he was doing me a favor when he unfollowed me, or else I would be conscious about his online presence forever. Our love story would never happen, and it did not happen, so why did I have to keep him seared in my mind? I made sure it did not happen, and I buried it down to the deepest depths and pretended I was better than my emotions. Because I was a coward, I was a creep (he’s the same age as my younger cousin), and I was a beaten mess of a dream.

People say time heals everything, which stands so strong, and I hope it will remain true for eternity. Because it did in the past. I don’t even think about Hayden anymore (except for now when I’m writing down these words), and the truth is, I don’t even remember his last name. I think it starts with P. I think about how silly I was with that silly crush, like a heat-of-the-moment kind of thing where you would recover and reflect on it a while later and ponder that he is not even that hot. Which is just what my mental health desperately needs. So, I do have hope for this. I hope I can get over this, which I absolutely and eventually will, but I will remember this time forever and how I am, for the umpteenth time, so down bad for a man who will never reciprocate my feelings. I wish to think it’s not because of my diffidence, because I tend to think things rationally, but it’s partially because of that. And I don’t know how long I can do this before we stop seeing each other regularly. Two more weeks from now, right? Yeah, yeah, yeah. One more meeting to record our final project and that will be it. I will. So why do I feel like this won’t end? Isn’t this a random project team assignment that ended up with me interacting with one of the most gorgeous men I’ve ever laid eyes on? If only I did not have to know such a person, my star would not be struck that brutally. It’s kind of wishful, really, since who knows—maybe in the next semester or next year’s time, I will be paired up with another beautiful man that I will swear is even more gorgeous than this one, and even be more down bad for, and he will once again unknowingly shatter my little brittle heart over and over again. So why am I even pretending, and why am I making this awkward? This stupid, stupid, uncontrollable homosexual heart. May I please, for the sake of G*d, have the power to just fully suppress it and pretend nothing ever happened? Nothing like a conversion camp, but enough to make my life less insufferable.

I know, I know. It will pass. It will go away. It will dwindle over time. I know magically as soon as next month I will look back on this and laugh about how dramatic I was about this matter, and in ten years’ time, he will become a shadow at the very distant corner of my memories. Somebody that I will be fond of with my brain and not ached by my heart, because again, time heals everything. My heart will still be vulnerable, because the last thing I want to do is to change myself for someone else who has little significance over my life, but at least I did not have to lift a single finger. I did not even have to pat myself on the shoulder for having “the willpower to not succumb to my desires.” What would I have even done? I would like to think I’m too old to be acting childishly and ask him out, but the truth is, I want to so badly. If only I had the courage. If only he sent me a signal. But I will not do it. Something that I will never have to wonder “what if?” because that “if” somehow never appeared. Let this be a lesson I will never forget. For I should have known that crushes will only crush you alive.

Maybe I was just so jobless that this matter took over my head this much. Damn, it truly sucks when you are single as fuck and your career goes nowhere. Look at what he is doing, I’m rationalizing every little thing again. Silly me. Fuck my life. At least Carly Rae Jepsen got my back for now.
