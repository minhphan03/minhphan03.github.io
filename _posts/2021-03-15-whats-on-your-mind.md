Is there any time that you have never thought of something when you’re conscious? Even when you tried not to think, you are processing the idea of trying not to think, and it was the only thing you were aware at that time. We, at any time, conceive about something. Something can be significant; something can be trivial. But we always have something crept into our minds at any time. When we are sleeping, dreams formed in our heads, as it may sound weird, lovely, or intimidating, we are wandering our minds to the portals of life.

Our minds are beautiful. It creates everything we know today, from the almost invisible nanotechnologies in chips to Mars exploration and the first black hole observation beyond the space thousands of light-years away. The human race has turned every impossibility to be possible and is still eager on that road. Science turned our world to wonders and created discoveries to convenience our lives. Art enriches our souls and blesses our already blooming natural beauties. We enchant the products of the human’s mind with obsession and detest, with appraisal and doubts. And the human race cannot do that without the inventions of people throughout hundreds of thousands of years throughout the existence of the human race.

Consider one thing: if the human mind cannot produce these irrational thoughts, would the world we live in ever exist? If Galileo Galilei didn’t risk his life on the line to announce his then-controversial hypothesis of the Earth’s orbit around the Sun and be accused of heresy, would the world we are living in ever be the same again? If Karl Marx didn’t propose socialism (which so many capitalists loathed insistently), would the world order remain the same? History only happens in one way, and according to the butterfly effect, if one thing deflected (just a little bit), things could have done in one could never imagine. With the beautiful indefinite curiosity of our minds, limiting freedom of thinking and speech is the last thing in line in my mind. Within oppression, there occurs protest and resistance. However, if we grant them freedom of speech, their minds will come up with more than just the limited thoughts of opposition. That a liberal country creates the most respected people; people should remind themselves what they were born to. Even if you were born at the bottom of society, it doesn’t mean that you are incapable of launching great things. When you limit your mind within the pitiful bounds of mediocre standards of race, class, and gender, it is the most outrageous: our brains are amorphous, our thoughts are fluid, and our minds are transcendent. It creeps through the narrowest corners of life, and it is what makes you become who you are today. Back to the basics, limiting what your mind can do is destructive to your existence and your concrete matters of fact. What exactly are you if you don’t think irrationally? Preprogrammed robots? Parrots?

We are living, not existing. Living is all about making yourself unique and memorable, and be conscious of who you are is a key to everything you want to be. Our minds are beautiful, so when you ask yourself, “What’s on your mind,” be honest with what is lingering in your head. People have crazy ideas, and crazy ideas fulfill our lives to unlock doors that no one ever thought to be opened. Don’t limit yourself within the boundaries of money and relatives, of jobs and marriage. Your mind can do more than that. The world will never be the same without you, and history could alter dramatically if your thoughts are not revealed.

Your mind is beautiful, twisted, and full of fantasy. Your soul is filled with surprises and hope. Just let your guard down and let your mind wander somewhere else, somewhere that you cannot grasp it when everyday life nonsense crammed your spirit. Reassure yourself that even when the harsh reality of life soaked your body, your mind is capable of ideas that you never thought before, questions that you never asked anyone before, and feelings that you never felt before. Once you reached that stage thinking outside the old box, you are emancipate yourself away from the mediocrity of life.
