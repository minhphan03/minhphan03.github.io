I’m writing about this on the course of my TOEFL class progress I’m studying right now, and the upcoming official test I will be talking in July.

———————–

To be honest, I am *just* a decent English learner. Rare use of advanced vocabulary, lack of cohesion/coherence in writing, suck at paraphrasing, improper use of grammar and got lost in listening lectures, that’s enough to say about my English situation I’m stuck in right now.

So I came back to my English center to sign up for a TOEFL class, determined to eradicate all of my past sufferings. In fact, that was exactly the class I got last year, so I could use my old textbooks to bring into class, but I didn’t. It was unfair and unacceptable to others in class, so I got my new handbooks after all.

The class elapsed in peace, sometimes boring. All I did in class was doing pre tests and practices, with some heating-up activities on the side. But homework is difference. We write our essays and submit them to the teacher and wait until he got all his marks on ’em. Haven’t been any physical red marks, I can submit and see my teacher’s green marks on my online paper after the announcement mails leap into my inbox, notifying me that someone has edited my document. And seem it isn’t interesting enough, after editing all of the papers, she sorted out the most problematic pieces and order us to edit it again (of course on the original paper).

Then it came my piece. Yes, the uttermost first belongs to me. And it was held publically so everyone could see it. Of course, I was ashamed and feeling attacked. I must have called it one of the most shameful moments of my life (until now), that I got to listen to everyone in that class that day criticizing and looking down on my abilities (the latter one is in my imagination, but apparently it was unavoidable). At first, my head was filling with red, anger and despair, indignation and agony, all fighting for the prime emotion reign in my head.

But after class, I came back to normal. Unappalled. My heart became light again. For a second, I never knew how come I could be that pressed and mad. Later came I ruminated the situation, figured it out, wiser than I initially thought. At that nick of time, I vaguely realized what is constructive critism.

To the authentic words (believe it or not is your choice) of [dictionary.net](dictionary.net), constructive criticism is “the process of offering valid and well-reasoned opinions about the work of others, usually involving both positive and negative comments, in a friendly manner rather than an oppositional one.” That in the same word of negativity on my homework essay, she did it with positivity manner and true guidance, with the attempt to help me but not to bash one of her students. After that day, she still act friendly and openly, with her wholehearted support. Now I know that constructive criticism is so essential not to me, but everyone else should receive it with open-mindness and willingness to co-construct themselves. Basically it’s different from bashing, so keep in mind that differentiating between the two takes time, but training yourselves into the immersive world of thoughts, it’s profoundly going to make advance.
