Tomorrow is officially Halloween and I don’t have and did not have anything exciting lately for the event lately. Am I just feeling so alone because even my roommate was feeling so contented being alone without going out of the room for days, and I could not justify his actions of feeling totally fine with it? I tried to asked him out to go to a movie screening with me but he would not budge and said that he was lazy, which was so lame of an excuse, and I tried with every fiber in my arm not to groan and scream my head out. 

For the love of G*d, I am about to talk about social media and how it seems like everybody else is having fun except me. I hate that I feel alone on a Saturday, but the remark is worsen on a “holiday” weekend. Like every laughter, every conversation I accidentally overheard was trying to torture me and killing me. Oh how I wanted to be a part of a crowd. Oh how I craved to feel as a whole in a conversation.

I know for a while now not to trust on expectations because I’m just going to be disappointed. I did not know that it would turn out to be THIS disappointing, and on another note, trapped in an unescapable world of lonesome. I was supposed to hang out with one of my friends to go to a Halloween themed park but she had an accident and could not go so I stayed home. 

I wrote a little bit about kitsch a while ago and I wonder if I am trying to tell myself I am fine by overspending and showing off the better part of myself to the world, deceiving the vulnerable me that I don’t really need anybody, that I’m contented on my own, that I’m an introvert and I would rather stay at home than go out. Isn’t it just a pathetic hoax? I cannot be even who I am and who I want myself to be. If everything is going to be alright for me right now, I probably am not satisfied. Who is so blinded by the images on the social media that he was crazy envious of other’s social life and bright smiles when they were standing with their friends. None other than me. Pellucidly me.

But it could have been much worse, I assume? My mood would be way sourer if my ACMS major decision was a rejection instead of an acceptance, or if my MacBook would not arrive on time, or if my dinner with my ex-flatmate was cancelled (I HOPE FOR THE LOVE OF G*D NO PLEASE) This week is a hotchpotch, a farrago of sorts if I have to describe. What expected to be ample with plans turned out to be not so handful. So I am here in my room blasting my music off at 9 PM praying for all these negative feelings to wither away as soon as possible. I’m putting “I Wanna Go” by Britney Spears on right now and it is a fucking mood oh my god.
