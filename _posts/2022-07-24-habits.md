Earlier today I was looking at somebody’s instagram screenshot on facebook and later when I came back, the screenshot was still on and instinctively I clicked on the activity tab on the picture. There was a red dot on the button (indicating that there was some new notifications). After I clicked on it did I belatedly realize that it was just a screenshot. I am absolutely horrified at how i was programmed to click on that button firstly every time I log on my social media. I was so desperate to check if anybody has interacted with me. At the same time, I intentionally watch myself falling for a sticky trap unable to escape, but how crazy it is to realize that I have done nothing so far to even attempt to prevent it from happening so soon. It belatedly dawned on my mind after so long how dependent and accustomed I had been on utilizing these platforms. I could not help but getting antsy after a day not checking Twitter, or if there was any new stuff on Instagram. The fact that there is an urge to check out these dots and clear them away is alarming and disturbing — and there is this subconciously accustomed thinking that somehow, after doing that, I would be granted something in return. Very Pavlovian of me [SIGHED].

Of course I did not come across it again, or else I would wreck my phone out of embarrassment. I went back to using my phone excessively and tapping on the Facebook icon at least five times a day despite not being very active on the app anymore. I foound myself seemingly open it out of a routine, like the idea that any day on the phone NOT touching it would be considered a weird day. That was even when I TURNED OFF all the notification badges on all the social media I engaged.
