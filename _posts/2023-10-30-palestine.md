I am not someone who cares a lot about politics, but when it comes to Palestine and Israel, it’s no longer a matter of policies and debate; it is a human rights issue. The war has escalated to an unprecedented level of violence and tragedy in the modern world. Is it different from Russia vs. Ukraine? Not that much, but we all know the main driving factor behind that war was pure political pressure. This one? No. This is a matter of national pride, self-determination, and liberation. Therefore, when I read this tweet, my first thought was, 'Were ALL students killed? What the actual fuck?' Can you believe that what the Israeli government is doing is essentially genocide? Over some land? They bombed the territory using white phosphorus, a prohibited chemical that causes long-hidden aftereffects such as cancer and 'successfully' wiped out an entire generation. A WHOLE generation of people, and at least a couple more whose lives are hanging by a thread under those ruins. And it’s not going to be the only chemical in the air! Dust from the ruins, uranium, and other toxic materials would slowly kill the whole population over the years. How hopeless it is to read about that in the news and not be able to do anything? I've read about cases of Palestinian people trying to contact their loved ones hiding in the territory, only to be cut off by an Internet blackout, followed by an actual electric blackout, as Israeli forces destroyed towers and lines. They knew that by cutting off communication, their bloody acts could somehow go unnoticed. This is sickening and nasty, and it's even more terrifying to know that we can do nothing but pray and helplessly witness the killings unfold.

As a Vietnamese whose country went through endless periods of war in the past, I was taught at a very young age that war was just a political device to achieve an ultimate 'goal', whether it was the Chinese invaders from the North claiming their local impact and seeking submission, or the French/US forces from the West exploiting natural and cheap labor resources for their capitalistic endeavors. Similarly, Israeli invasions are just another display of power and a pathetic attempt to erase history. Their game plan is identical to that of the US or the British—invade Palestinian land, steal land, then establish a government on that land just because they have power and weapons. As the Palestinian male population diminishes due to constant war efforts, women, elders, and children are left behind, now doomed to the same 'fate.' Their final days of total blackout, without aid and an idea of why they were at the end target of these attacks. Without food, without clean water, without meds, without peace of mind, and forever damaged with unfixable trauma. They would never have known if they would be dead in the next minute, barely containing themselves waiting for the final breath to come at any time. Death became a getaway, a comfortable escape from the woes and oppression they would never get rid of as long as Palestine was a valid country on the map. How heartbreaking it is to see your historical landmarks and settlements go up in flames and be stolen by outsiders, while your existence and culture are erased minute by minute. It's terror right in front of our eyes, unfolding without anyone stepping in to reverse the consequences. All this happened because of one small group of people's Islamophobia and thousands of others either ignoring what's happening or silently supporting this mindset. What has the world truly come to?

It's so disheartening how public and private institutions privately fund these activities or do nothing to address this situation or the inhumane acts that the Israeli government has committed. I am disillusioned and terrified at how we have become so accustomed to these war crimes and violence happening around the world every day, despite what we have been through in the past. The discrimination, belittlement, oppression, and enabling of these acts against these poor victims disgust me and make me lose hope in the world day by day. Did you know that the said government was spreading propaganda against Hamas, spreading lies on the damage they’ve done while stripping the skin off collected Palestinian bodies into their skin banks without consent, like caged animals waiting to be slaughtered? I know we are too selfish, too preoccupied in our bubbles to care about what's happening out there, but please just take a second to breathe, look around, and witness how twisted and unstable our lives have become. The people who benefit from these acts of killing are powerful enough to survive attacks on the main population, so never think they would come and protect you. Media outlets never covered the marches and protests because these went against their benefits (in the sense of powerful forces of Israeli connections in the Western political system) and “violated” the propaganda. So much for free speech. Therefore, it's now high time you stood up and supported what you believe in. Don't just ignore what's happening. Even if you are not chronically online like me, you must know that what is happening these days in Gaza, Congo, and Myanmar is inhumane and must be stopped.

UW (my school) had some Free Palestine protests during the past few weeks, and while they did not make that much noise, I am still thankful that people cared enough about human rights to organize and call for action. Of course, an immediate ceasefire is what we are demanding right now, but we also want to call off the US-backed siege on Israel (the government’s been supplying ammunition for the bombings), as well as the school’s anti-Palestinian stance towards its own faculty, students, and ties towards ammunition companies. There are so many things we, as normal citizens, can do to show our solidarity with Palestinian lives and advocate for change other than donating funds for humanitarian aid and rescue. For instance, we should boycott corporations who are aiding or enabling genocide. Starbucks, McDonalds, Puma, Siemens, and X Corp. (Twitter) are just some of the biggest names that shown their incentives to donate funds to Israel. If you want to stand on the right side of history, please consider stop supporting and using the services/products from these companies. I know it’s difficult to escape their impact on our lives considering how big they are (I need to quit Twitter, for example), but please be a smart consumer and practice ethical consumerism. Search for alternatives (which may be less convenient for you and may take a while to get used to adapt behaviors, but you’ll thank your choices later), and boycott brands to show your support. Don’t feel bad for already bought their products, as long as you swear and remember not to continue supporting their businesses in the future. Those are the easiest actions you can do right away — cutting costs and engaging in conscious consumption is always good for your pockets — and only they only require you to shift your habits a little bit day by day. The immediate impact is promising: [Starbucks lost $11 billion in its value within days](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/international-business/starbucks-loses-11-billion-amidst-boycotts-due-to-its-support-for-israel/articleshow/105800958.cms?from=mdr), and [McDonalds allegedly began to lose customers in Muslim-dominant countries like Malaysia](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/boycott-campaigns-over-gaza-war-hit-western-brands-some-arab-countries-2023-11-22/). Past evidences show that [these attempts may not work in the long run](https://theconversation.com/israel-why-the-brand-boycotts-probably-wont-make-much-difference-217125) after the heat is over, as so many people’s lives are dependent on these corporations, but any type of publicity is good, and corporations are forced to publicize their intentions with statements, and depending on those statements can we tear them down for good. 

I myself don’t drink Starbucks or eat McDonalds anymore, so boycotting them is no issue for me. It’s the other brands that donated privately that is for me. I hate to find out that the Centaphil brand I’m using for my skincare routine is using my money to aid genocide causes, that Apple (where I’m currently subscribing to for a variety of services) is discreetly enabling genocides to continuing taking place. We know that there are many talented Israeli people currently working and controlling these corporations that these donations are bound to happen, and I wish they would take a minute to themselves and think about what they were doing. They are indirectly repeating what happened to millions of their ancestors eighty years ago. Why is war still have to be a thing, and power hungry people still have to raise hell over peoples’ lives just for them to be the villains in millions and millions of others’ eyes. I hate that there are still those who are enabling and letting these things happen. Privilege is such a crazy concept to acknowledge, and sympathy unfortunately made way for hatred and division in this fragile world. Everyone has goddamn opinions. Noone is willing to listen. Why can’t people realize that killing and hating is not the answer to whatever is happening in our world? Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ageism, xenophobia, ableism, etc. could be avoided if we all collectively learn to empathize and appreciate each others’ individuality and power to inspire others. To let this genocide, or any genocide, in Kenya, Congo, China, among others, go on is our failure as a human race and as a society.

To think that to simply exist is a privilege to so many lives is heart-wretching. Lives were gone and families were displaced out of spite from the people they had no business or control over. My heart goes out to these innocent victims, to children whose dreams remain unfulfilled and hopes shattered, to speechless mothers holding their children's corpses in their hands, and to the people whose lives were tormented and crushed under the bloody hands of terrorism, genocide, and “ethnic cleansing.” I do not mean that we must condemn Jews because hatred can never be a good solution, but we need to shift our focus to gather attention and show people how the Palestinian community depends on us right in this moment. Do whatever you can in your capability to show your support and stand on the right side of history. Show these media outlets that we cannot be silenced. In this day and age, we can get resources from anywhere, so educate yourself and do what you believe is just. We did it during the Vietnam War time, where public outcry made way to peace and resolution for a self-determination issue. If we can repeat history and bring an end to all of this, let us do what we do right now. Palestinians need you. War victims need you. Marginal communities need you. #FreePalestine
