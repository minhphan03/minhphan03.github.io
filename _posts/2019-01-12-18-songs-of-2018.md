**18\. Lost in Japan (Remix) - Shawn Mendes & Zedd**

**1\. Venice Bitch – Lana Del Rey**

“Why could this song deserve the top spot?” The answer is, firstly, that it was the first song that came up in my mind when I decided to make the list. It is, once again not to make a bias, never from the desire to bring my idol to shine and block others from doing so. Lana Del Rey, with this masterpiece, deserves this spot because of these 3 elements:

* The unconventionality: Who wants to hear a 10-minute long ass song when it is initially made not for long enough? There it is, the experimental came to play. It’s uneasy to conclude if it is successful enough, but to me, it is. 
* The unexceptional length of the instrumental part: You know how long it is? Almost 6 minutes, and that’s almost two times longer than a normal pop song! What’s the deal of it? The feels are! It’s nothing but feels and nostalgia, but golden remembrance of the 80s and the congratulations for Lana’s mind.
* The content of the song: A Venice Bitch is, according to Genius, is a play on words from “Venice Beach” — both may mispronounce from the one to the other, from the obscene name to the place of bohemian culture, artistic murals and street stands. It reminisces the young aspiration of the youths, dreaming and craving the future in the lands of opportunities and desire to prove themselves. The Venice bitch is that, healing all the golden era from the past from the dusty, cruel and unemotional present day.

Fav lyrics: *You're in the yard, I light the fire / And as the summer fades away / Nothing gold can stay*
