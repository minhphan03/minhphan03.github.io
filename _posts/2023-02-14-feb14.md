I don’t know what’s wrong with me. I hate the way my emotions are so volatile (turn for the worse, of course) and there is nothing I can do about it. Earlier today I met a guy from a class I knew and we definitely talked before (chatting a lil bit) and then we parted ways. Today I said hi to him and he said hi to me and that’s it. I did not say anything else and I felt super awkward. He was with a girl that he knew since forever and I felt like an intruder to their conversation so I did not know what to say so I left them there. I felt bad because I didn’t attempt to continue the conversation, and I think that it was so bad of me that these pitiful thoughts just keep rewinding back in my mind. I knew that I could do something but I just felt like a shell, rejecting these solutions and decided that I could just go back to the way I was before: pretending that I have never known him before. And it could have been something better. Something that my pitiful mind can accept, of course, as a defeat of sorts. Fucking sad, I tell you that.

I hate how small things like that ruined my day, when it should be just a part of life… like, I don’t know, relationships can end and fail as you go. It’s a normal thing, or it is something that I haven’t tried enough. Maybe one day I will talk to him again, and I can just pretend that these feelings has never occurred. But how crazy I am to pretend?

I just thought that my mood lately has been very much summed up in irritation. I’m irritated with everyone and everybody. I think that it’s so cruel. Or maybe because today is Valentine’s and I don’t want to do anything with it. I hate how pathetic and reclusive I am. But I’m so fucking lonely and no one seems to understand me. Except my therapist but we’re still in that ‘getting to know each other’ kinda phase and I still hesitate sometimes to talk through stuff with her. 

I know that I am like a shell of who I am but it is just who I am. I overthink a lot about everything and nothing. I think a lot about what people think of me and sometimes it’s just suffocating and unbearable. I don’t think my last year self can even recognize who I am today, and I am being very honest. What the actual fuck is happening? Who is the one typing these words right at this moment?

I went to therapy having self-doubt whether my mental case was serious enough to need treatment, like do I actually have depression? But now I feel like I am having it, and I can’t seem to talk about it with anyone. Are we all ok, or are we just pretenders painting on perfect masks to hide away the vulnerable, fucked up minds?

I tell myself again and again that life will change and everything will be nothingness and all of my busy contemplations will turn to tidbits between no one — no one cares and no one will remember, unless I wake up one day remember this day out of nowhere — and I could care less about it. Oh how time has changed. 

You know about the war. I was just reading about Dang Thuy Tram and her diary. It was so sad, not because of the war itself, but because she would never be able to recall the diary because she died during the war. Reading her entries, I thought about how she did not have to deal with these mundane thoughts and anxiety about her relationships with other, as they were bonded through the war efforts: the trauma, the death of their loved ones, the wounded soldiers, the hopelessness of the war, and the fight against their enemies. I wish I would die for a greater cause and not because of my insufferable, unfixable insanity.

Just a fucking bullshit kind of day. And it is Valentine’s. How convenient!
