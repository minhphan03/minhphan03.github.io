In just about half an hour, a new page is turned to. So it should be the best of me to recount all of what “massive achievements” (to me) I’ve done in 2018 — the year of many big events and disasters and crises of the world.

For being a gifted school, it takes tons of time and effort to study to have a chance to step back, even if you’re gifted enough. Otherwise, the “backdoor” is there to save you, but doing it by yourself is inevitably better than spending money, right?

I feel like every time I go to Starbucks, I really make a lesson out to me over conversations and relationships. Not to mention that the merchandise are so attractive and beautifully made, aren’t them?

3. (Maybe it’s not a big deal for many people but to) **adhere an organization (YLO) is really a achievement** I can finally see myself through working with other people and have the spur tomove forward and show everybody else that I HAVE A ROLE IN MY ENVIRONMENT. Being alone and unconnected is so annoying and not suggested so to be able to break out of my comfort zone and do things that I’ve never done before is such a amendment.

4. **For being a Table Topic Master in a ISB Gavel event**, just once, is my confrontationof the fear of public speaking to the potential change of myself. I used to be fearful to public speaking because I hate the feeling of being criticized and the worry that everyone might not be satisfied with my speech. Thanks to a friend, I had the chance to prevail and overcome it, and now it’s a complete turn-over. There’s no fear, and there’s no hesitation to publicthis website.

Thank you for 2018,

Minh.
