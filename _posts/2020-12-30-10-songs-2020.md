This year has been unconventional. In fact, it is disastrous for many people. Regardless, I am thankful for challenges that have shaped me to become the person I am today. I had experience money shortage, living long distance away from my family at 16, and the first ever pandemic that prepare me for more struggles to appear in the future. Because of the sharp, abrupt change in lifestyle, this year’s music market is not the exception. Artists like Taylor Swift has broken her two-year album cycle to release folklore and evermore; Ariana Grande’s positions is her third-in-a-row album, and Adele is nowhere in sight. I’m picking ten favorite songs released this year, a tradition that recurred annually since the inception of my website. Let’s start!

**10\. Camila Cambello – “Dream Of You”**

This is my absolute gem from her sophomore album Romance because it’s oozing love. This is kind of like a whole love letter from her to her boyfriend Shawn Mendes (in which he reciprocated in his Wonder‘s track Dream.) A great (but unfortunately too overproduced) ballad blended so organically with her angelic voice, which is persuasively shown in her high notes in the choruses of the song “All I do since I met you is dream of you,” indicating her utmost desire to love her partner unconditionally.


Fav lyrics: *All I do, the whole day through, is dream of you / All I do, since I met you, is dream of you*

**9\. Ariana Grande – “My Hair”**

The standout track from her sixth studio album positions is about her iconic ponytail, in which many people used to bring out to complain that she never changed her image. Like the hair, this song is iconic. It showcases her vocal capabilities that rarely anyone can compare. The future diva of our age Ari has delivered much satisfying, skip-less, eargasmic whispers that makes her the icon today. She must have great pride in her ponytail, because this track alone, to me, is better than the remaining thirteen tracks combined. It is not about her hair only: it is also about her pride, her vulnerability that a new love would ruin her reputation, which is empathetic especially when we consider how she had gone through much tragedy in the past with her lovers.

Fav lyrics: *I want you to touch it softly / Like the way you do my mind / It got body and it’s smooth to touch / The same way as my skin*

**8\. Bleachers Featuring Bruce Springsteen – “Chinatown”**

Jack Antonoff slowly becomes my favorite producer ever. His music catalogue is immaculate, if you know what songs he have (co-)written and produced: Lana Del Rey’s NFR, Taylor Swift’s Cruel Summer, and a couple of songs from Lorde, all three my favorite artists. When his band announced the new single Chinatown featuring Bruce Springsteen, I had to check it out. It was a perfect 70s style summery track, brimming with reveries and youthfulness, that I could sing all day daydreaming about my non-existent lover, riding with him in a car beside on the beach road (such infatuation, but never mind.) The heat, the energy of the song radiates warmth and passion through my body every time I listen to it, because it’s what a good summer song has to deliver. Antonoff’s distinct voice harmonizes with Bruce’s raspy voice beautifully, along with guitar chords recall the old, nostalgic feelings of the iconic 70s’ rock style that established the names of Rolling Stones or Led Zeppelin.


Fav lyrics: *We don’t have to break every dream we’ve ever known /Running down the hallway / Running up the long road*

**7\. Little Mix – “Break Up Song”**

Another 80’s inspired song in this list comes from my favorite talented girls! Little Mix is so underrated, it’s unfair, because Break Up Song is one of their better songs in their new album Confetti, if it isn’t the best. I guess it’s synth-pop elements, which has brought these girls to the fame through songs like Black Magic and Shout Out To My Ex, is now back with its full charm-on power. But coated with the 80s style synths, the first single from the album is more than just a dancing bop: it emits nostalgia and creativity. This is such a great comeback from Little Mix, but the general public has let down our girls once again, because the commercial success of the song is nowhere near their those of the previous eras. But no use of crying over spoilt milk — it’s time to turn it up and play it on and play it on…


Fav lyrics: *I don’t wanna turn back time’ / Cause what’s another lonely night? / I know under these lights, I’m good without you*

**6\. Lady Gaga & Ariana Grande – “Rain On Me”**

This is the gay anthem of 2020 — an EDM dance song for the gays to get through the ups and downs of this year. Lady Gaga has always been the icon of the LGBTQIA+ community, and the single collaborated with the powerhouse Ariana Grande is expected to get the attention of the community. When Gaga sang “I didn’t ask for the rainfall” in the first verse, we all feel it: the fact that 2020 started with all optimism of a new decade and three months later, a pandemic bulldozed it to pieces. But later, when she and Ari harmonized “at least I’m alive,” a positive note on survival after all, it becomes lighter. We gotta dance the fear away, and by delivering such a positive message over a light, bop-py, banging song, Gaga made it clear to us: no matter what happens, just be yourself and shake everything off. Being alive is celebrated and precious enough.


I had to endure my first birthday away from home, but I had spent that night swaying to this song alone in my bedroom, and it was more than needed to light up my spirit again


Fav lyrics: *I didn’t ask for a free ride / I only asked you to show me a real good time / I never asked for the rainfall / At least I showed up, you showed me nothing at all*

**6\. Halsey – “Ashley”**

Ashley is Halsey’s real name, in case you don’t know. So you can guess who she is singing about in this song. It is about herself. The first track of her 2020’s album Manic delves into the idea that Ashley, Halsey’s real being behind the limelights, is just a human, and she has flaws. She was suffered from mental illness and hysteria. She was alone in the mess that she somehow made it out of herself to be because of fame and popularity. She had manic, she had issues that only through music could she describe her feelings. The song perfectly encapsulates it: “I don’t wanna be someone in America just fighting the hysteria.” She talks about her disappointment and helplessness in fighting for the help of such a heartless, individualistic and sociopolitically negative country, in which the vulnerability is proactively expressed to show that Ashley, who is just a normal woman in a society, also suffered like anybody else, that when she is no longer alive, the only thing she wants to leave is the “dust,” the legacy that she had created: nothing else. Isn’t that what we always want to leave behind, when money becomes valueless?


Fav lyrics: *Someday, someday, when I burst into flames / I’ll leave you the dust, my love / Hope a bit of it’ll be enough to help remember the / Days when we came to this place*

**4\. Carly Rae Jepsen – “Felt This Way”**

Another pop, chilling, dance-y, hearty, poppy, feathery track in her “side B” album Dedicated: Side B is such a fun experiment, to begin with. Having almost the same lyrics but different tempos, the double-hitter “Felt This Way” and “Stay Away” both talks about feelings towards a lover. While the latter is more of the physical side, the former is about feathery feelings of lovers having obsession over each other’s touch and affection. I really love how light and vulnerable her voice sounds when she sings the choruses “Oh, if it was only an attraction / How come nobody ever felt this way?” Wow, that was such an ethereal experience that transcended me to the heaven gates, really. Check it out for yourself, because despite there’s nothing remarkable to say about the lyrics, the production and vocals are so outta this world it has to land into my list.


I initially had to fight over this song and “Comeback” featuring Bleachers, another jam I had on repeat for a while when it was released, on the same album. But I thought that for production-wise, this is better, because Antonoff’s style, despite very alluring and addictive, is not as lighthearted as this one (I guess?)


Fav lyrics: *And if my love’s too strong for you, walk away / But I can’t make this wrong when I see your face / My home is your body, how can I stay away?*

**3\. Taylor Swift – “Tolerate It”**

Taylor Swift’s track 5s are well-known to be deeply vulnerable, and tolerate it is not the exception. Unlike other track 5s that depicts her stories, tolerate it is her penning a character — a wife — struggling to get attention (love) from her husband. In the song, listeners can feel how breaking Taylor’s vocals is, as she goes through the song talking about how desperate the character begging to become his centerfold in the marriage. In his life, she was frivolous; a nothingness, an empty line in his glorious profile. How patronizing and painful is that? But once again, how talented the songwriter is to captivate our emotions into a nonexistent, nameless character? Only Taylor Swift can do it.


Fav lyrics: *If it’s all in my head, tell me now / Tell me I’ve got it wrong somehow / I know my love should be celebrated / But you tolerate it*

**2\. Troye Sivan – “Rager Teenager”**

Sivan’s new EP in a dream is one of the few music projects that I absolutely love every track, so obviously it is really time-consuming to single out a single song to represent this whole album into this list, but eventually it is here. Rager teenager is a promotional single of the EP, and what enthralls me in this masterpiece is how incandescent, infatuated and insane he elicits in the song to reflect the topic of the song: “rager” is to enrage, to exhibit rapture and vigor in sexuality. The song itself is rapturous and frenzy at times but also dreamy in places, just like Sivan’s inner teenage self trying to stifle rebelliousness.


Fav lyrics: I *just wanna go wild (Wild, wild) / I just wanna do some shit just to try / In your car tonight / In your bed tonight*

**1\. Taylor Swift – “This Is Me Trying”**

This pandemic has been wild, but in the end what remains is conversations and bad memories. We have persist this capricious year with our naked, vulnerable heart, so let’s shout out ourselves for persevere this upheaval and conclude this list with the best song of the year (in my opinion): this is me trying, Taylor Swift’s message to all of us out here fighting to stay sane. Please remind yourself that “at least I’m trying” instead of giving in to negativity. Remain positive during hard times can be difficult, but it is not impossible. Exposure to social media with now much unnecessary free time may render you insecure, but focus on yourself and your mental health is a good practice. Be kind to yourself, and believe that you’re trying to be better.


Fav lyrics: *I was so ahead of the curve, the curve became a sphere / Fell behind all my classmates and I ended up here*
