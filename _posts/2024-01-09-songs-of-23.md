Per our tradition, we are back with another year-end list of my favorite songs released this year. I intially planned to write only for the top 15, but there were two songs I discovered after making up the list that I really wanted to include in the list, so I added them and now we have 17. Enjoy reading!

**17\. get him back - Olivia Rodrigo**

To be frank, I was not that impressed with the track until I watched the music video, which elevated the song to another dimension. It finally dawned on me how quirky, bold, and chaotic a teenager can be, and it’s so fun and fresh that we can just be care-free in this goddamn depressing world for a minute and be our weirdest self, mulling over the most trivial things like “he said he’s 6’2 and I said ‘dude, nice try!’” or “I want to kiss his face with an uppercut!” The double meaning of the phrase “get him back” was so brilliant on her end, and showcased her ability as a songwriter and a lyricist. I know I know we should have been over this discussion a long time ago, but she reminded me of Taylor so much. The song was just so freaking entertaining and such an earworm that it filled me with joy everytime I listened to it. God I love the lyrics so much. Sometimes you don’t need cut-deep, full-on-SAT-1600-vocabulary, emotional lyrics to get emotions out of your listeners. Quirky, eccentric, punchline-brimmed ones will suffice, and in this case, damn she did the song justice. Also the drums went nutty in this one. She truly has a knack for rock-inspired pop songs.

Fav lyrics: "I met a guy in the summer and I left him in the spring / He argued with me about everything / He had an ego and a temper and a wandering eye / He said he's six-foot-two, and I'm like, 'Dude, nice try'"

**16\. Smoke - Caroline Polachek**

I recently discovered Caroline Polachek through her frequent collaborator Charli XCX, but she already left me quite an impressive impression. Her voice is so unique, especially on her single “So Hot You’re Hurting My Feelings” in which she used her voice as the instrumental for the bridge. She did it again in “Smoke,” a standout from her sophomore album “Desire, I Want to Turn Into You.” Super interesting production, super addictive, carthatic outro, and arguably the best song of the whole album for me. She’s criminally underrated as an artist, and “Smoke” was only one of many songs in her distinct discography that deserved more attention.

Fav lyrics: "And you are / Melting everything about me, oh / Don't worry about me / It's just smoke"

**15\. I like u - Tove Lo**

“DISTORTED VOCALS ALERT YES MAMA SLAY” was what I first exclaimed (in my head) when I listened to this song for the first time. A true dance-floor, club banger for the girls and the gays, I like u reminds me of a much-needed release after a heartbreak (while the song apparently told the story of a hopeless crush/pining after somebody). Tove Lo was truly gifted as a songwriter writing dance and electronic tunes like this. The Swedish pop singer’s Y2K-inspired track had a hook 2 Die 4 (wink-wink), and glittery in all the right places. I always lowkey vibe to sad songs with upbeat tunes, just because.

Fav lyrics: "I run into you everywhere / But you push me away / Does it mean you're still with her? / She convinced you to stay?"

**14\. Angel - PinkPantheress**

Like many other people, my first exposure to PinkPantheress was through her collaboration with Ice Spice, “Boy’s a liar Pt.2” and of course what stood out for me was firstly, her baby voice. It somewhat reminded me of Britney during her debut, which was kind of nice, but the instrumentals of that song was more interesting. Garage pop, Y2K-inspired tune was lovely and naughty, flirty as well as catchy. However, it is just an ok track: I would listen to it, but I would not OBSESS over. The lyrics was kind of corny too, if you think about it. Therefore, when I heard “Angel” for the first time, I was very surprised. It totally blew me away! The Y2K vibes were still there, but with l production credits from the one and only BloodPop, it was here and there, and it was everywhere in my head. Another epitome of saxophone immensely upgrade the song in the bag, guys. I’ve checked out her new album recently, and this song is still my favorite of hers. It’s just so good.

Fav lyrics: "'Cause one day / One day, my baby just went away / My angel / You're what haunts me now that you're away"

**13\. Stadium Love - Carly Rae Jepsen**

Grandiose, loud, and unabashedly romantic, "Stadium Love" is Carly in her signature elements: conquering pop in all its most positive, upbeat, and cheesiest self. The lyricism may not be strong on this one, but who cares about lyrics on a song where its drum-filled school-band style rock beat is enough to move you to the moon and back? I do not especially like rock, but some synths and rocking basses are more than welcome to blast on the speakers, especially when you want to scream “HEY LOOK, NOW I'M ONLY FEELING STADIUM LOVE” along with her. What a cathartic moment you only get on songs like “Happier Than Ever” or “I Know The End,” which are also excellent tracks on their performers’ discographies!

Fav lyrics: "I got you on my, ooh, not enough / Got you on my mind but it's not enough / Give me stadium love, stadium love, stadium love (watch this)"

**12\. Needs - Tinashe**

Tinashe was never an artist you can get into on your first try, at least for me. It happened to me many times, even with her most approachable project “Songs for You” (I only listen to her independent projects). The same went with Needs. I could only warm up to the song after the fifth time, and it was during watching the music video for it. I felt bad for not being able to fully appreciate “Talk To Me Nice,” the lead single of her new project "BB/ANG3L," and it made me hesitate to check out Needs. I’m so glad I did. Tinashe is truly an inspiring artist. Going independent, establishing her own label, and constantly experimenting and trying out new sounds, she is the role model to me. Just like how she sang, “We all got needs” (to idolize someone, i guess) in her perfect, sultry, sexy vocals of hers.

Fav lyrics: "He's simpin' / Said, 'Lord, please forgive me,' oh / Said, 'I don't like beggin' but I'm on my knees'"

**11. Xúc Cảm Bộ Máy - Cá Hồi Hoang feat. Phùng Khánh Linh**

I always felt obliged to include a Vietnamese song in these year-end lists just because I am a Vietnamese and I listen to Vietnamese music sometimes, but this time, it does not feel so. The decision came effortless: “Xúc Cảm Bộ Máy” earns its spot right here. I don’t listen to Cá Hồi Hoang, but I know it’s a very popular indie band, and this remix with Phùng Khánh Linh was the final gift for the fans before its disband (they did not mention a disband, but you know when they said along the lines of “hey, we decided this is going to be our last album as a band” you can guess it). Anyway, this song truly hits different when you apply it to the context of the band. It asks the questions of tomorrow “and then we rethink about tomorrow, about the next days, where are we heading?” and attempts a promise of reunion “this heartbeat here remains a continuing belief, for us to know that we still have love to not be a soulless robot” (pardon my terrible translation). You know, it always hurts bearing the end of something you sear too closely to heart, wondering what is going to happen once it’s gone. It is going to be fine eventually, just like the sunset and sunrise will bring light back to earth after a period of darkness.

Fav lyrics: "Nhịp đập ở đây chính là niềm tin vẫn còn / Để ta biết / Mình còn tình yêu hay là người máy vô hồn / Để ta biết / Nhịp đập ở đây, ở đây, ở đây"

**10\. Jaded - Miley Cyrus**

I kept going back and forth choosing between “River” and “Jaded” to put in this list, just because both songs were so freaking good. The former was more upbeat with dance-pop and synth-pop influences with an addictive yet short electric guitar solo in the bridge that drove me crazy for three days straight, but in the long run, the latter was simply a better song. It aged like fine wine, and it showcased Miley’s talent as a vocalist. You cannot go wrong with a ballad most of the time, and this is a perfect case. Simple, gorgeous, and rock-influenced, the song gave a stunning first impression with a buildup to the chorus that ever since then, every time the chorus came on I was ready to scream (silently) “I’M SORRY THAT YOU’RE JADED” with the most dramatic expression like I was actually the one breaking up with Liam Hemsworth. 

Fav lyrics: "And it's a fucking shame that it ended like that / You broke your own heart, but you'd never say that / We went to Hell, but we never came back"

**9\. Tastes Like You - Ellie Goulding**

Ellie Goulding released what I believed was the best pop album this year with Higher Than Heaven, with bangers after bangers. It’s kind of odd that while the album singles pulled me in to listen to the album, the non-singles were the ones on repeat most of the time. “Tastes Like You,” track 14 and part of the deluxe edition, were booming, energetic, and vehement. It’s undeniably and unequivocally love-ridden (in a negative way, if you happen to listen to the song itself). Bittersweet lyrics about the aftertaste of a breakup in a clubby tune, again, that’s my favorite genre. 

Fav lyrics: "So I'm gonna raise one more glass to the times that we had / I know that we're both happier, it's true / But the heartache still tastes like you"

**8\. Is It Over Now? (Taylor's Version) [From The Vault] - Taylor Swift**

When Taylor Swift revealed that the vault songs on her version of the juggernaut that was the 1989 album were “insane,” she did not really lie, because “Is It Over Now?” was a certified bop! Not a career highlight like "Style" or "Wildest Dreams," but "Is It Over Now?" had the signature Taylor gut-wrenching, cut-through-glass lyrics that hits right where it should be, and a bonkers bridge that again reinforced her status of “I am an architect I’m drawing up the plans.” It wasn’t helping when I saw the TikTok dance on Twitter of people dancing to the bridge, and it’s damed if those lyrics didn’t help the song to perform today critically and commercially. I just love when she rap-sings, because she can do so many things with that pen of hers. I also really dig the subtle “squeaks” and “weird blares” (I don't know what they are called ell oh ell) and echoes in the production throughout the song as it added pretty cool touches and single it out from the rest of the vault tracks.

Fav lyrics: "At least I had the decency / To keep my nights out of sight / Only rumors 'bout my hips and thighs / And my whispered sighs"

I would argue that Olivia Rodrigo shines better in her upbeat songs, like "ballad of a homeschooled girl" or "brutal" or "good 4 u", but no one can deny that piano ballads were what propelled her to stardom in the first place, as drivers license spent eight weeks at no 1 in Billboard Hot 100 right from its debut, defining one of the most successful career openings for any artists ever. the grudge sounds somewhat similar to drivers license, as [many fans discussed](https://www.reddit.com/r/OliviaRodrigo/comments/1709iyo/the_grudge_drivers_license/) and in a way it was nostalgic enough to pull me in. The lyrics were sharp, pointed, and tear-jerking. A career highlight for me, honestly.

**6\. Fishtail - Lana Del Rey**

My attitude towards autotune varies case by case, but miss Del Rey never misses with her usage of the technique in her songs "Fishtail," a sultry trap-fused masterpiece about a souring relationship, found Lana questioning her man’s feelings through his intentions. In the song, we saw retrospective imagery that were Lana’s signature: palm trees in black and white, technicolor, summer bruises, victorian nightgowns, and definitely LA. The title being a synophone to “fish tales,” an idiom of an exaggerated or not truthful story, vindicates this notion. What was he doing here if he could not even doing the bare minimum braiding her hair? Anyways, maybe I’m looking too deep into things that aren’t real. The lyrics are ambiguous enough to sound like they are not that serious, that it’s simply a song about braiding fishtails after all. 

Fav lyrics: "For me, you are the one / And if I'm not the one for you / Don't just say it"

**5\. Shadow - Carly Rae Jepsen**

If there is one thing that Carly Rae Jepsen cannot do, it is her incapability to make a bad song (so far)” are roughly the words I repeat every time I put one of her songs on any of my year-end list (last year was an example). I’m ecstatic to say that this tradition is aging like fine wine, as “Shadow” made it to this year’s list (along with "Stadium Love" mentioned earlier). Poppy, dance-y, yet different from all the elements Jepsen experimented before, her airy vocals also gave this song a huge boost. A vibe I can’t describe. It’s transitional, maybe: a restful, chilly, content morning coffee on the balcony or on the kitchen island getting ready and excited to explore a new day ahead outside of your cocoon? It’s a stand-out on the album, at least for me (while others may insist that Psychedelic Switch or So Right are way better), more so because you got Danielle Haim on the drums and Rostam on the production. No one can escape that!

Fav lyrics: "The architecture of this connection / Is your reflection is always followin' me / City to city, heartbeat to heartbeat / Body to body (Baby)"

**4\. What Was I Made For? - Billie Eilish**

Billie’s most popular original soundtrack, “No Time To Die,” written for the 007 Movie, was one of the most popular movie soundtracks in 2021. This year, it is “What Was I Made For?” for the Barbie movie. Barbie already made a lasting commercial and cultural impact, but I’m sure that even if the movie does not exist and somehow Billie was still able to write the exact same song, I strongly believe that the song would still make waves on the charts. It’s tearjerking, gloomy, and existential (thankfully not nihilism) that complements perfectly with the movie. In the song, Eilish likens herself to the dolls, wondering over and over the question “what was I made for?” over the strip-back, almost acoustic piano instrumental. As a celebrity, she became a model and ideal figure for so many teenage girls around the world, constantly having to upkeep her image and alive in the eyes for those who pay for and invest in her music and concerts (music listeners and label executives). Considering how unconventional her music career is, I understand thoroughly Eilish’s constant pressure to maintain her fragile reputation in the industry while evolving her image and exploring concepts to stay relevant as a female musician.

Fav lyrics: "Think I forgot how to be happy / Something I'm not, but something I can be / Something I wait for"

**3\. How To Stay With You - Troye Sivan**

Choosing a song from Sivan’s newest studio album to put on this list was a demanding task. The thought process went from “What’s the Time Where You Are?” to “Still Got It” to “Can’t Go Back, Baby” and finalized to “How To Stay With You.” This song is truly a grower. I listened to it for the first time when the album came out and did not revisit until a month later. I was up to an awakening that time. That saxophone was no joke. From then, it was always on my repeat, as I repent for all the lost time I should have listened to the song. It’s quite jazzy, despite I have no clue what it means. A vibe, a chill, low-light-in-the-late-evening-before-bed vibe, despite the song lyrics have nothing to do with it. I take it for inducing reminiscence, if it wasn’t for yearning. 

Fav lyrics: "Baby, turn around, give me one more kiss / I'm a little bit lost on how to stay with you"

**2\. Let The Light In - Lana Del Rey feat. Father John Misty**

I don’t think there is much to say about this masterpiece: it sounds so lovely, heartwarming, and beautiful. In a way, this is a more approachable, pop-friendlier song in Lana’s latest album Did you know there was a tunnel under Ocean Blvd than most, aside from the last three songs. It’s undeniable ballad, a touch of acoustic guitar but not too stripped down thanks to enriched instrumentals, mainly with piano and strings. Many fans said it sounds super nostalgic, which I guess a part has to do with Father John Misty’s vocals. Their harmonization sounds something from the past that mixed gender groups would always convey in a way — ABBA, for example? We don’t really see it nowadays, not with John Misty’s deep vocals that only experienced musicians would possess and not young pop/rock stars. Others said it sounds like a Beatles song. I myself haven’t properly listened to any of the legendary band’s music, but if that was what she intended to do, it wouldn’t have come at a surprise, after all, because she referenced them right in the lyrics.

Fav lyrics: "Got my dress on tight 'cause you know that I / Look shinin' in the light, there's so much ridin' / On this life and how we write our love song"

**1\. Just For You - Ellie Goulding**

I don’t have anything to say much about this song, because I feel like my words are too limited to describe how brilliant and addictive this masterpiece of a pop song is. Its groovy flow as smooth as butter, Ellie’s vocals shining as always, and the chorus… just outta this world! Regarded by many as the most emotional song on the album, the song elicits a euphoric feeling, while at the same time calming and floating on air. To me, it is a perfect song to put on in any occasion: while chilling, in the club, taking showers, or even studying (sometimes). AND THE SYNTHS YES THE SYNTHS. Thanks to the underlaying vocals and instrumentals, the songs feels like going on forever and forever (in a good way), maintaining the vibe it carries while synthesizing with Ellie’s masterful vocal techniques (reverb drenching). When the chorus stopped and the instrumentals kept going on and echoing back and forth, that was when I knew this song was a hidden gem. It’s just so difficult to find a song nowadays where the choruses flow flawlessly into the verses like that. Just to give you another detail, unlike the other top songs on the previous year-end lists, Just For You happens to be my most played song of the year, confirming the catchiness I kept coming back for. 

Fav lyrics: "Yeah, I've got a heartbeat just for you / Just for you, just for you / I've got a real thing just for you"
