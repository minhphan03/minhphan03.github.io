WHOO-OOOH! Running fast as hell, 2019 is not only going to an end, but it is also going to finish the whole DECADE, the 2010s. Still, this is a chaotic decade, with emerging problems arising, from climate change affecting the sea level to species extinction with koalas being the newest member as “functionally extinct” listed; further political strife between the US and China to continuing war in the Middle East. Hopeless as we could say, there are new advances that improve people’s lives and daily activities, as well as new horizons people fueled into their own existing societies and around the globe. Music, not taking for granted, witnessed new changes and fresh air, creating a giant leap for the old giants the latest achievements to gain and new records to break. Within the emersion of music streaming, people discover new artists quickly and, without a doubt, make the industry more diverse and open for modern influences from aside. Cross-cultural music is not a new thing: trap and hip-hop becomes viral, Latin music makes a bold step, and artists start to dig up new inspirations from their cultural roots. Annually, I make my top year-end songs (which based on my taste), and this year is not the exception. So, let’s get down into this!

**9\. Tứ Phủ - Hoàng Thuỳ Linh**

By the 10th anniversary in the music industry, Linh launched her third album, Hoang, and immediately it received critical acclaim from music lovers. Tu Phu, her second single from the album, is one of the reasons why. Inspired by the strength of women and the rituals of mother goddesses worshipping in Vietnam. In the song, she sings about the disparity towards women and their melancholy in seeking their own love and happiness. The addictive beat from Triple D and splendid lyrics from Ho Hoai Anh also blended beautifully with Linh’s vocals, made it into the top 9 this year :).

Fav lyrics: *Đào phai mấy kiếp thân em đoạ đày/ Đời gập ghềnh thuyền tình chòng chành chao nghiêng*

**8\. Lose You To Love Me – Selena Gomez**

Served as the lead single from her upcoming album Rare (2020), Selena reminisces back to her past relationships, delivered her admission of cutting her lover off to gain back her self-loving. Her sultry, layered vocals diluted into the dreamy piano chords and the result? A beautifully written pop ballad that is undoubtedly suitable for a lead single (risky as it may seem, the song did well on the charts)

Fav Lyrics: *Set fires to my forest / And you let it burn / Sang off-key in my chorus / ‘Cause it wasn’t yours*

**7\. Future Nostalgia – Dua Lipa**

This is one of the songs that blew my mind right from the name. “Future” and “Nostalgia” seems like two distinct, contrasting objects, but it feels so brandy in here. The self-titled album song from DUA LIPA, as it is named, created a whole futuristic mood but still conveys a sense of familiarity in it: female empowerment on the background of turntable sounds and electronic catchy tunes that complement each other beautifully, on a degree that gave it the honorable chance to stand out in the market of mediocre pop songs nowadays.

Fave lyrics: *Can’t beat a Rolling Stone if you live in a glass house (Future nostalgia) / You keep on talking that talk, one day you’re gonna blast out.*

**6\. Find U Again – Mark Ronson Featuring Camila Cabello**

The synth-pop track from Mark’s newest album “Late Night Feelings” is such a mood because it produced such a “late-night feeling” with melodic chords and bass-infused drops that permeated (in a sensual yet enervating) through Camila’s extraordinary vocals. To me, it achieved the goal of a song that brought in a new multi-dimensional poppy track that is negligently easy made as a “basic” music work from an EDM producer. In fact, Mark isn’t that kinda guy. The “Uptown Funk” producer really made the song into my top 10 this time.

Fav lyrics: *I’ll try to pass the night away with somebody new / But they don’t have a shot when I compare them to you*

**5\. The greatest – Lana Del Rey**

If this song isn’t listed as one of the best songs released this year, I’ll absolutely go insane – how meaningful it is, let alone the nostalgic tunes from grand piano vibes, that deserved to be in critics’ top lists – in prestigious Pitchfork’s Year-End Top 100 Best Songs at #2, and Vulture’s Best Song of 2019. The beauty of this song comes from the ubiquitous topic musicians often mention nostalgia and remembrance to good old days of the music industry. Lana also made a full step by singing how the culture itself of the Americans, as a whole, was blending so well with the musical reception. She also referred to David Bowie’s ‘Life on Mars,’ as well as the California forest fires in 2018 and the Beach Boys.

Fav lyrics: *The culture is lit and if this is it, I had a ball / I guess that I’m burned out after all*

**4\. ghostin – Ariana Grande**

This underrated gem from Ariana’s lastest album ‘thank u, next,’ talked about her relationship with her ex-boyfriend, rapper ‘Mac Miller’, who committed suicide from suffering to alcohol addiction and depression. With her haunting, vulnerable vocals, she was open wide to talk about her sadness, melancholy, and regrets for seeing him wretched with uncared and his addiction, but couldn’t do anything about it. She ‘ghosted’ him in pain, unwanted emotions, and had to let him go for good – all for his future and hers, too.

Fav lyrics: *Look at the cards that we’ve been dealt / If you were anybody else / Probably wouldn’t last a day / Every tear’s a rain parade from hell (From hell)*

**3\. Now That I Found You – Carly Rae Jepsen**

Unlike ‘ghostin’, the second single from Carly’s ‘Dedicated’ made it into my top 3 (ME! wished) as a bubblegum pop (with no bias). With poppy, energetic, positive, and captivating tunes, Carly radiated her talent in making previous viral pop songs (‘I Really Like You’ and ‘Call Me Maybe’) into this track. In fact, it was so well-produced and positive that, in fact, every time I hear it, I have to dance to it non-stop, and that’s hơ magical it is to be líted in my top 3 of 2019 songs!

Fav lyrics: *Thought it was impossible / It’s just like a miracle / Say nothing’s impossible now*

**2\. Cornelia Street – Taylor Swift**

Despite being entirely in love in her newest album ‘Lover,’ Taylor feels a sense of vulnerability and fragility of her passion that she had, thinking that one day her lover would leave him and go away, with Taylor left in heartbroken and melancholy. That she found her true love and made it last long was a factor that kept her feeling so alarming – how all the storm and criticism that would surround and drown her again into her helplessness and despondence.

Fav lyrics: *I don’t wanna lose you, hope it never ends /I’d never walk Cornelia Street again*

**1\. Cinnamon Girl – Lana Del Rey**

Cinnamon Girl is not a girl made from cinnamon – in fact, cinnamon is a taste, in which its exquisite taste give a feeling of danger and surreptitiousness of a couple in love – and when I looked at the title song, I have to laugh because of how weird it was. Despite so, the song was pretty dope, with Lana’s rare whistle and vocal cords in the post-chorus of the song; that feeling of climax and heavenly pleasantry was inherent lustrous and delivered a taste of delicacy and hope at the same time. We love how it ended up, the unvocalized chords at the end of the song left a sense of persistence and continuity – maybe that’s how the relationship would continue – lonely, painful, and dark…

Fav lyrics: *There’re things I wanna say to you, but I’ll just let you live / Like if you hold me without hurting me / You’ll be the first who ever did*
