1. take a fucking long shower. sometimes it will help you figure out a lot of things and time to contemplate. I kinda figured out what topic to present in a shower this Tuesday. however, do that only if you can pay for excessive use of water.
2. do homework. straight to the point. finish any of em if you have any.
3. learn something new. you don’t actually have to apply what you just learned right away, knowing it is enough. browse subreddits like todayilearn or explainlikeimfive. cool facts are waiting for you.
4. watch movies/ something on youtube (if you ran out of things watching on youtube, no you didn’t — the algorithms of youtube is good enough to not running out of recommendations). also you can read comics or books. 
5. online “window shopping” on online shopping platforms (don’t buy anything: if you want to kill your time, look for stuff you want to buy and compare prices on different platforms. save you money for later decisions). or you can do some bidding on eBay if you want (also kill your time). well you’re not losing any money while bidding. only bid the ones that you’re definitely losing lol. don’t use amazon cuz i hate monopolies, also amazon deals are pretty straightforward so you cannot kill as much time as you want
6. workout in your room. plank, sit up, push up, etc. just find some workout routines that you can do at home.
7. write something: pen up your impending thoughts and write what you want to write. it does not have to be good… 
8. use social media. self-explanatory. use it until you’re exhausted and don’t think that it’s worth browsing anymore. literally anything to kill your time
9. search for meme (vids/pics). make yourself some laugh today hunny you deserve it.
10. hate people. idk some people need to be hated more i swear. post some drama / hot takes on your stories/ twitter. you don’t need engagements, and you can delete it any time you want.
11. eat your food. while eating, admire your food and be grateful that you have something to eat instead of having to go out.
12. update your accounts: sometimes you need to clean up a little bit. update your linkedin profile, change passwords, make sure nobody is using your netflix illegally / without consent, delete accounts that you don’t use anymore to prevent leaking passwords.
13. take a bunch of selfies/ pictures and spend time hating on them / editing them.
14. play games. i only know y8 (for online games) but you can play whatever you want. i’m sure if you don’t like games no you’re lying. there must be something you like.
15. practice your instruments if you have any. i’m not talented enough but idk maybe you are.
16. sleep. idk how but if you can maximize your sleep time there is less chance you will run out of stuff to do.
17. talk to strangers. idk omegle, discord, meetup.com.
18. listen to music / blasting it out loud in the room. make it booming. dance, cry, throwing up, banging your head, etc.
19. talk to a person you haven’t talk to in a while. can be a distant friend, relative. 
20. talking to yourself. can be very emotional and carthatic. contemplate on your issues. instead of trying to submerge them, make them valid. cry, throwing up, etc. again… ignore the fucking world outside. learn to love yourself first. it’s the most important thing. speak out loud. i swear nobody fucking cares.
21. make plans on what you’re gonna do when you’re out of this isolation and all of the preventive measures to not turn urself in this way afuckinggain.
22. write a resume for yourself/cover letter then be depressed about how crazy the job market is and sleep. very tear-inducing and sleep-inducing. so true babeh.
