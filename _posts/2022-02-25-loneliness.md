*for some reasons this piece has been sitting in my drafts since last year’s Thanksgiving, but it is here now! I did not edit anything since then, so these are all raw emotions*

TW: self-harm, suicide, depression

I feel like everything is about to burst out, and there’s nothing to do to stop it. It’s just that I feel like I’m the most useless person in the world, and to be honest, there is nothing I can do. I’m just going to break down right now.

Thanksgiving is here, and I was so excited because I made plans with one of my coworkers that we would celebrate a Friendsgiving together by going to Capitol Hill and enjoying a fanciful dinner together. It’s not like you can go to a fancy place like Capitol Hill every day to have dinner with another friend, so I didn’t want to miss this. I anticipated that it would be canceled (by some great hunch I had), and boy was it magical. It started with a text I sent her earlier today, but she said she had a migraine and decided to sleep until like 5 PM today, so it was apparent we had to cancel. I told her then that maybe we could meet the following day, but then she said that she was supposed to have a ‘date’ date and I know that I shouldn’t insist on her canceling her day to go out with me because no sound person would do that. I was like, “you know what, I don’t want to feel like I have to be pitiful, all alone on myself on a remarkable night like this. I’m not going to beg her no more.” So I told her to cancel it altogether and go to the date instead. I’m being very generous here, and I know that I shouldn’t feel sad because I sacrificed my fun to let her recover from the migraine and enjoy her holidays her way, but I can’t help feeling down. 

I was so happy because my roommates had been out since yesterday to go back to get home and celebrate Thanksgiving. Unfortunately, it was only a fleeting moment. I slowly realized that everybody else was having fun out there, if not staying with their family, and there’s me in here alone doing nothing, eating junk food and frozen dishes. This is peak loneliness. I’m just tired. I mean… we aren’t born to be alone. We’re humans: we are social creatures. It’s so funny, you know, that college is the best way for you to communicate and open up your soul to meet people and make friends, but I’m just in here staying alone in my room ranting on this.

I learned the hard way that you’re bound to be lonely at some point in your life, even as a college student, and I think that’s it so very important for me to recognize, first and foremost. ALRIGHT, so I haven’t started reading this book yet, but the author stated that half of the new students felt lonely in the first 12 months of their college life. Personally, that was somewhat exaggerated, but maybe it’s true. I don’t see it happening around me — hence the number may be lower (at least for me because I’m a perfect symbol for that). I’d think that it is encouraging for us to realize that thing because despite how many activities and opportunities schools provide for us to participate in and make new friends, it’s easier on paper than in reality — students have to be more approachable themselves complete the mission. And we are not guaranteed that all the people we meet at an event will cross paths again. I do think that you should know that you’re bound to feel lonely at some point. For example, I don’t have another significant person, I don’t have a very close friend (at least not right here), and I’m slipping away from anybody else that I have ever known) so fundamentally I have to restart my inner circles again from scratch. I’m helpless. Well, isn’t it so lovely to have that imprinted in my mind every second? 

OK, that’s enough for my vent today… I don’t want to say that I have come to terms with realizing that I’m just going to be a lonely person from now on… I’m still trying to process it and swallow the truth that my emotions and how I want to experience my life right now depend heavily on relationships rather than on my own as a very dependent person. Colleges provide very smooth yet rare opportunities to develop genuine relationships, which are significantly more difficult afterward. The more I depend on people and open up my soul, the better I would have guaranteed a better college experience (or just like life expectations). But all I want to do right now is to close myself off again. I’m just tired. Whatever I do, it’s not enough; whatever I want, life’s not going to fulfill, so what’s the point of trying anymore? I’m tired of shit. I just want to sleep. I just want to d-word. I just want to k-word myself. I know it’s pathetic, but as I said, I’m this close to breaking down. I don’t want to be so dependent on other people because they constantly let me down every day and mess me up in the worst way possible. It’s so dark, I know. I’m just trying to speak it out, to let them all out, to realize, and to admit that I’m not just a human and had thresholds to endure. 

Oh my God, I just want to drop out of school, just want to go back, sleep, and stop doing anything at all. To pretend the world does not exist. To wake up tomorrow and see that everything is still under my control. That I can still look at the world out there without feeling sorry for myself. Every time I walk past people, seeing them smiling, talking to the friends and savoring happiness in their conversation bubbles, I was so jealous I could not breathe. bitch. bitch. dammit.

Tomorrow is another day, but right now…

I cannot do this shit anymore.
