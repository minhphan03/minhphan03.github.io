I have been looking for some research opportunities and I know that it is easier to ask (and apply for some) than to find jobs but my flashbacks from the past keep haunting me. I feel disconnected and hopeless sending out cold emails again asking for something that I don’t think I can do good at (research in labs) and that it would render me diffident. I have done compiling out my introductory email template but I decided to take a break until I met with the undergraduate research advisor on Tuesday asking about it. I don’t know what to do in the meantime. Should I have come up with a list of people to send out already? Oh yeah I should but somehow I just keep procrastinating. The feeling being in the middle of something unfinished irks me to no end. I want to scream. I want to scream where noone can hear me that I am just drowning in hopelessness and I can never achieve what I (and my daddy) want me to be. Music does not appeal me anymore, which scares me. I’m listening to Red Taylor’s Version which was just released yesterday but I am definitely in no mood for it, like I’m just numb. I don’t want to compare myself to anyone but like everyone says, it’s easier said than done. There are so many people out there that could have done better than me and I’m feeling like an imposter trying to not to give up and drown myself in this rigorous whirlpool. Being an international student is so hard. You have every obstacle known to student. You have no choice but to participate in every opportunity possible to build up your resume and make up for your limited time of legal employment in the US. What a sad ugly tragic affair. The halloween parties were over now and I have never been more grateful for that to happen, but every week seems to bear new challenges to hurdle. 

I am hopelessly waiting for a rejection letter from a research lab I had applied to. They said they would respond in an average of four days but it’s been four days and I haven’t seen anything so should I send something? Oh yeah I definitely should. But I can’t bear myself to do that just now. I’m completely burnt out for all the wrong reasons. Not school affairs anymore but just my future in general. Where will I go after this? Where will my next breakdown crash? 

I should have learned from my blood, sweat and tears that I should not hole up in the room for too long because I would just begin to question every single event that happened in the day, and today is not an exception. I should have been overfilled with joy. Taylor’s new record was such a blessing to this boring hellhole of a week, and Britney Spears is finally free from her thirteen-year conservatorship. Even earlier today I posted a story saying that nothing could possibly go wrong for today, but well — never overestimate the power of teenage angst. I swore to myself that I would not let it affect me, but no it has to at any cost.

— UPDATE: I got accepted to the lab but there was like 120 people (and more going in) and that was a fucking lot of people in a lab, and I fear to be shunned and not being able to contribute anything to the projects. I’m having second thoughts now. Oh deary me! Well let me just continue cold-emailing professors. I know I’m selfish but obviously if I don’t enjoy that experience it would just impact my productivity. That should better be a back-up plan only.
