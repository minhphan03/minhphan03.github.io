I don’t know what to say: I’m officially seventeen. I guess for everybody else it’s just a number, but to me it is a milestone that I’ll never forget. I assume the reason why I overreacted over this number is because I one of my greatest fears is aging. Of course we want to stay young, to stay beautiful forever. I want to stay young forever because I guess I’ll never be mentally prepared enough to deal with adultery and adult issues, as known as jobs, money issues, relationships, etc.

They said that I was not eighteen yet, so there’s nothing to make a fuss. I don’t think so. I acknowledge the fact that I’m aging every freaking day, and it’s expected that I have to be mature at some point (SOME POINT.) People can’t expect me to be a fun, childlike person all the time — it’s exhausting and hollow. I guess the reality rendered us “hyper-mature” (a word I want to coin, witnessing that most children are mature too soon in this day and age) and it’s pitiful. Children have to end their childhood so abruptly because of pressures from the society and parents and school. Internet is just a haven for anxiety. The more frequently children use social media platforms, the more continuously they compare themselves with others, and it’s a pressure of growing up to acknowledge life, to be mature and know everything to survive in a harsh life competition.

I don’t know what to say, because now I’m seventeen I cannot freeze time at seventeen. I can’t stop maturing because it’s nature. But I wish people will not have anxiety for being old like me. Just enjoy yourself and embrace your character. Age is not important! (I’ll try to imprint this) I repeat: Age is not important! It’s just the birthday that emphasize my aging process, so I kind of freaked out a little bit.

Meanwhile, I guess I’ll just wish a great seventeen age with happy memories 🙂 and constantly put Seventeen by Alessia Cara on repeat for this beautiful new age. Hope 2021 is going to be better and I have a bf by then…

Minh x
