Another year is about to pass. How cliche is it to ask oneself how in this witchcraft another year is approaching so early? O-ho, it is definitely cliche, but not more than writing one of my favorite pieces of the year — the top songs of the year. If you haven’t already know, by tradition, it is time for a wrap up for my music section. For this year’s top songs, let me present 15 songs released this year that have accompanied me through the ups and downs of a very mixed 2021 (or should you say, 2020.2)

**15\. I Drink Wine – Adele**

There’s a reason why Adele is eulogized by so many as “Queen of Heartbreak,” and the highlight “I Drink Wine” in her recently released project “30” fully encapsulates her power of transforming the emotions of divorce into beautifully sang lyrics. With soul-brimmed melodies and powerful vocals, she mulls over her inner worldview of a brokenhearted woman sipping wine in complete silence, who, in the highest pitch possible, wailing over her ill-fated marriage. Only being drunk could a woman relieve all the weights she had carried on for so long.

Fav lyrics: *Why am I obsessing / About the things I can’t control / Why am I seeking approval / From people I don’t even know?*

**14\. Amoeba – Clairo**

Clairo is a phenomenal songwriter, and “Amoeba” is a living evidence. The opening track to “Sling,” her sophomore album, introduces us to a less-teenage-more-adult version of Claire Cottrill compared to the experimental, distinct style of “Immunity.” Deeper, more insightful perspective into adulthood as well as domesticity complexities that we all crave at some point, the groovy song telltales the journey of Clairo herself leaving the newfound fame and lively crowds and coming back to her own roots (“You haven’t called your family twice / I can hope tonight goes diffеrently / But I show up to the party just to leavе.”) Amidst the ups and downs that the pandemic caused for our humanity, maybe (just maybe) the best way to be at complete ease is to prioritize the right things (in this case, family and friends).

Fav lyrics: *Pulling back, I tried to find / The point of wasting precious time / I sip and toast to normalcy / A fool’s way into jealousy*

**13\. Working For The Knife – Mitski**

Among songs that depict either love, money or mental issues (which seems to be on trend during the pandemic), “Working for the Knife” stands out to talk about our current state of capitalism. Desperation to strive for success and recognition leaves people burning out in tirades, and we haven’t discussed that enough. In a world where social media are full of people boasting themselves, we feel obligated to strive for the same standing and put behind what we really need – navigation. We work hard, play hard but forget where we are, just like how Mitski described herself on the verge of thirty: “I used to think I’d be done by twenty / Now at twenty-nine, the road ahead appears the same.” It is not a matter of oneself anymore but a whole generation getting stuck in achieving the same success that their predecessors have managed to do.

Fav lyrics: *I always thought the choice was mine / And I was right but I just chose wrong / I start the day lying and end with the truth /That I’m dying for the knife*

**12\. Mr. Perfectly Fine – Taylor Swift**

Part of the reasons why Taylor Swift decided to rerecord her catalogue was to give the fans an ultimate listening experience to all of the initial songs she planned to put on her records, and those include “vault tracks” — never-released before songs that Swift scrapped off her albums. “Mr. Perfectly Fine” was one of the standout vault tracks from Fearless (Taylor’s Version) and it successfully showcases her brilliant songwriting with 27 sarcastic “Mr.’s”, calling the man out on being so “nice” to her, as well as the playful, upbeat tunes and production from Jack Antonoff. I guess that the song got scrapped because of many of these reasons: too problematic (Swift with newfound star fame has enough criticism and tabloid), or too pop (which Fearless was not meant to be, or maybe it was just Antonoff’s production style), or not yet finished at that time. 

Fav lyrics: *Cause I hear he’s got his arm ’round a brand-new girl / I’ve been pickin’ up my heart, he’s been pickin’ up her / And I never got past what you put me through / But it’s wonderful to see that it never phased you*

**11\. The Path – Lorde**

Melodrama resonated so in tune with my emotions for a lot of reasons, and “The Path”, the opening track from Lorde’s third album, is not the exception. Filled with a more downtempo, more mysterious and folkier sound, “The Path” introduces us to a brand new side of Lorde, who is now successfully detached and unfettered from the disturbances of a teenager. In the track we found new wisdom as she settled down out from the limelights, completely away from “Melodrama.” More spiritually and brightly charged than ever, she dismissed herself as a cause of people’s sadness and agony, resulted from the songs that she had written in the past, and led them to trust their own fate more “Let’s hope the sun will show us the path.” By declaring so, Ella was ready to branch into a brand new sound in “Solar Power,” regarded controversial in her fandom, that she was sure she was meant to make one (her fate).

Fav lyrics: *Now if you’re looking for a saviour, well, that’s not me / You need someone to take your pain for you? / Well, that’s not me*

**10\. Brutal – Olivia Rodrigo**

What’s better than a song that represents your age that is also coincidentally released the same year you are at that age? “brutal,” the rock-infused opening track from the emerging popstar Olivia Rodrigo is THAT track. (you know what I mean?) The cathartic drum-fused instrumentals and relatable lyrics of an antsy seventeen-year-old teenager having to sort through her life painted a perfect description of my seventeenth year on this planet. Every time the song comes on, I always put off what I was doing and rocked my body to it, just like a teenager in a 2000s rom-com movie. 

Fav lyrics: *And I’m so sick of 17 / Where’s my fucking teenage dream? / If someone tells me one more time / ‘Enjoy your youth,’ I’m gonna cry*

**9\. You Right – Doja Cat Featuring The Weeknd**

This song was everywhere on the radio for a reason. It checked all the boxes needed for a hit nowadays: catchy hooks, dope lyrics, fiery rap verses, and sick featured artist(s). The Weeknd, who was on the prime this year, paled compared to Doja Cat and it was not because he was featured. If you knew me for years, you know I’m not a big fan of hip-hop, but she was a gladly welcomed exception: she blended in pop and hip-hop so incredibly skillfully, first from “Say So” and “Kiss Me More” and now with “You Right” that gave hip-hop a more enjoyable listening experience for me. This is the Doja Cat world we’re living in, and she served us bops and bops time after time. Check out “Planet Her,” her latest studio album, if you could.

Fav lyrics: *You’d be thinkin’ ’bout it every day / Don’t believe in fairytales, but we got our fantasies*

**8\. Happier Than Ever - Billie Eilish**

Billie’s always been known as more of a ballad, slow-tempo producing artist, with sleepy hits like “ocean eyes” and “when the party’s over.” Even “bad guy,” a more uptempo track, was delivered with breathy and whisper vocals, so you must imagine how blown out I was when I first heard the latter half of “Happier Than Ever,” the title track of her sophomore album, when it first released. Powerful yet exasperating vocals, bombarding layers of drums, bass and screaming, and the relief of escaping a toxic relationship (hence “Happier Than Ever”). I still remembered that experience, and I knew that it brought out how promising her capabilities and future can be. To me, this is the magnum opus that she needed to redefine her catalogue and the impression that the general public had on her. I cannot wait to see what she has next in store for her third album: will she return to her own safe roots, or will she embrace a new completely different route at her own risk, but would renew her appeal?

Fav lyrics: *So don’t waste the time I don’t have / And don’t try to make me feel bad / I could talk about every time that you showed up on time / But I’d have an empty line ’cause you never did*

**7\. Cherry Blossom – Lana Del Rey**

I don’t normally listen to leaks unless artists have no intentions to release them anytime soon because I want to show respect to their hardworking creative processes, but “Cherry Blossom” is a (quite embarrassing) outlier. Ever since Lana teased the lyrics of the song on one of her Instagram posts, I was curious and checked it out on YouTube, and I knew right away after finishing the first chorus that it was a crime she had not released the song by then. However, what a pleasant surprise life can be when she finally decided to put it on Blue Banisters, her latest studio album and one of the two she put out this year (hardworking queen). Even more powerful than romantic love is maternal love, and Lana showcased this side of hers in Cherry Blossom with heartwarming yet tormenting lyrics interweaving in  melodious piano chords. Mommy issues and motherhood have always been a recognizable topic in many of her interviews, but this is the first time she decided to express her aspiration to be a mother to recompense her childhood. 

Fav lyrics: *And when you’re scared, I’ll be right here / You feel afraid, mommy is there / It’s a cruel, cruel world*

**6\. Undo (Back To My Heart) – Tinashe**

Tinashe Kachingwe is an insultingly underrated artist, and her newest project 333 again proves how potential she can be as an independent artist. Focused on the theme of self-love (hence the number of angel 333 as the title) and healing, the project is indeed an interesting and refreshing record to listen to. Undo (Back to My Heart), the 9th track on the album, is a hidden gem. The dance workout track resuscitates a gloomy end of summer (this year, specifically), showing her powerful vocals and steamy, sultry lyrics that had all the elements needed to be a viral hit. Longing of the heart is what we all refuse to accept, but if there is a song that speaks up your mind, this is it. Let go of all your self-centeredness and concede your feelings once in a while and you would feel lighter in no time. Some of the songs that you should also check out in this album include “X (feat. Jeremiah)”, “The Chase” and “Angels.”

Fav lyrics: *Gotta just let go of our pride (Pride) / Lookin’ for some peace of mind / Everything for me is mine / But I realize it just isn’t right*

**5\. Between Us - Little Mix**

I have taken for granted many things in my life, but I’m for sure never less proud about Little Mix, one of the most successful girl bands in the history, celebrated their 10th year anniversary this year. “Between Us,” the newest album by the trio Leigh Anne-Pinnock, Jade Thirlwall and Perrie Edwards is a hit compilation celebrating their journey, blood, sweat and tears working in the music industry. The title track is unquestionably about their friendship, the elemental characteristic that they have always prided themselves on — the long-lasting, close-knit sisterhood — and embraced in the last ten years. Listening to the song, immersing myself in their harmonizing vocals together and having my tears brimmed when they referred their previous songs in the lyrics were enough to affirm my belief that they are here to stay and will only be stronger from now on, both as individuals and as a group. 

Fav lyrics: *So, here’s my vow / Starting from herе and now / Nothing comes between us*

**4\. Halley’s Comet – Billie Eilish**

Billie Eilish O’Connell, one of the most notable faces of the new generation artists, further proved her capabilities (to me) by not only have one but TWO songs from the album to be featured on this year-end list. Halley’s Comet is just a simple piano ballad, but it was so cleverly and nicely produced that it did not only fit Billie’s vocals but also the theme of the album: morose, nervous nights of growing up and facing uncertainties. It was an experience listening to this song: it lulled me to “sleep”, but in this “sleep” came the dream of fulfilling love and crave of affection, and ended with a distinct outro with Eilish’s signature distorted vocals admitting that she “might have fallen in love,” like she never wanted to leave that dream for fear that she could never fall for anybody else again. Because she knew that love is as rare as to see the Halley’s Comet.

Fav lyrics: *I was good at feeling nothin’, now I’m hopeless / What a drag to love you like I do*

**3\. Yosemite – Lana Del Rey**

I don’t know how Lana and Jack did it but they absolutely DID what’s needed to be done with Yosemite, because it was SUCH a mood. Deserted, sandy heat of the Midwest area; cold winds through dirty boots and jeans; loving in freedom, in style, and in certainty among aching lovers in the wild with nobody to judge; and the notion that love can stand the test of time, are depicted through the lens of a Wild West movie was what I imagined every time I listened to this song. The masterpiece magically captured such a specific image for me to mull over every time I wander away from the restraints of cityscapes. Like Halley’s Comet, it’s just a piano ballad, but the folky elements (like acoustic guitars, breezes, wind bells, and bongos) definitely added so much depth into the song. The spoken bridge reinstates the growth of said love, of a forevermore, happy ending to the love being tested against publicity and distance. It is my most played song of 2021, and hands down it truly deserves to be there.

Fav lyrics: *We did it for fun, we did it for free / When I was young till eternity / I’ll do it for the right reasons / Withstanding all the time, changes, and seasons*

**2\. All Too Well (10 Minute Version) (Taylor’s Version)(From The Vault) -  Taylor Swift**

A fan favorite right from the get-go, All Too Well is the loudest and most persuasive attestation for Swift’s songwriting capabilities, with powerful yet heartbreaking lyrics and echoing tunes. And, as a gift for fans, she decided to put the original ten minute version of the song into the rerelease of Red (Taylor’s Version), and despite not being a single and went against the standard formula of a successful hit nowadays, it got groundbreaking commercial success as well as critical acclaim. She depicted her affair with Jake Gyllenhaal so beautifully and vividly through metaphors and imageries, painting autumn scenes of a beautiful afresh love which somehow ended up frosting and wilting in the worst possible way. The additional lyrics and verses somehow alleviated the listening experience without making it feel like I was listening to a ten minute song — and that’s how talented and experienced Swift is to manage to do so. This is her magnum opus, set her in stone of a legend status in the music industry.

Fav lyrics: *And you call me up again just to break me like a promise / So casually cruel in the name of being honest / I’m a crumpled up piece of paper lying here / ‘Cause I remember it all, all, all / Too well*

**1\. 1121 – Halsey**

I am absolutely not kidding to crown this song my all-year 2021-released favorite, because Halsey totally deserves this spot with 1121 (she was sitting on a throne in the album cover -- she knew she was a queen). What is the meaning behind the number, though? 11/21/2020 was the day Ashley found out that she was pregnant, and the whole song was all but just a vow about her unconditional love and sacrifice for the baby inside her body (at that time). Motherhood is a powerful and admirable concept e not all can experience, but she was able to conquer and do anything to stand by her son. Excruciating and intense vocals of the choruses, in my opinion, stood for the white hot affection to the human inside her, and the more quiet verses between were self-reminders of what being earlier said — the balance was too charming to not get stuck inside my head for days. Also, thanks to reading through the lyrics every time I listened to the song, I could not help but respecting her of how devoted she was with this bond, and admiring more than ever the passionate loving that only a mother and a child can achieve.

Fav lyrics: *Well, I won’t die for love / But ever since I met you / You could have my heart / And I would break it for you*
