A thing: life will end. Everything will burn down to ashes, and nothing will last forever. Someday I will watch somebody fall down to their knees and ended their lives right in front of me, and there will be nothing I can do to avoid it, to reverse its events. People would only value their life before they passed away, and it’s devastating what they had dreamed to accomplish before they are gone. Things that they had longed for fixing to make everything better, to make their life even more enjoyable.

Just like everything else it life, love will not always cover itself in exhilaration and the pink. It will go away in miserable, heartbreaking fate, in resignation and regrets. Someday that the most ecstatic fantasy cannot make it up for the lost that we all die for. I don’t want to lose it, I don’t want to look at the work in grey, in the dark, monotonous hues of black and white. Those colors are lifeless and deadly. Would life turn from pink to grey in mere seconds, when the devastating pierces of fate bleed you out till you die from having your heart wretched in isolation, in desolation and depression. People do not assign pink and white and black into emotions, but fate does. Fate can make you feel pinky, make you feel lively and in ultra exhilaration when it can, but can pull you to the bottomless abyss of despondency in a lifetime.

People always look for love. That’s what they do. They can experience love in its pure honeymoon phase, to be a fool in its surreal magic and then fate interjects and turn it upside down. If your love moves far away from you, would it seem to you that fate is betraying you? Is it preventing you from making pure bliss from your surroundings, from your emotions? Is fate so powerful and tragic at the same time to steal everything away from you, to pull them away from you so instantly that when your life finally stabilized and you looked back at the past, everything would be an unfixable mess. Love is a mutual, two-sided communication. When one cut it off, then it’s not the same anymore. No one will clean the mess except you.

I’m scared of what fate can do. Fate may bring a person to color my life in whatever shades it wants. It can be pink, in a fateful way, or in pure black of darkness and misery. Fate can do everything. Fate can destroy anyone. I’m scared of looking for love, because it will break me down. People look for love because they are ships looking for harbors. But when they anchored at the wrong harbor, everything would perish. I’m afraid of love because it is so fateful people would die for it. But I’m a human, and human always look for emotional support. When all the heroes in my life disappear, who will be my harbor? No one. Life would then turn to chaos, and I don’t want to experience it.

Not every love story would have a happy ending. It is not a Wattpad story where you can meet a werewolf that is destined with you for the fuck’s sake such thing as fate and turn your life into pinkness. It is not a rom-com movie that you can daydream everyday waiting for the prince/princess of your dreams appear out of nowhere to marry you. Reality it is. Fate manipulates all. If you meet the right person, everything falls into place… And if you fall for the wrong piece, then everything falls into pieces. Out of place. And even when you meet the right person, you have to fight for it. There’s no such thing as fated love. You have to build it.

Humans are mayflies rushing headlong into building love. Love is blinded, they said. Because love makes you feel like a champion, you readily pour your heart out to get it. And when you crash, the game ends. Will you replay the movie of your love story, the diners, the dates, the sleepovers, the kisses forever, or will you hear everybody else’s advice to  move on right away? If you choose the latter, you are not investing in your love, you are not believing in your fate. When your love becomes something that you regard it as a product, a bliss of fate, then it is something that you are truly experiencing as it is. People are mayflies, so they pour out their hearts non-stop until they burned into ashes, and love ended. Only when you believe that your love is a product of fate, that fate has brought you two together and challenged you to keep it undamaged, then that’s what genuine love is. It is something that you will break down if you lose it. It is something that when it is stolen by fate, you will cry for years. It is something that you are proud because you have spend time to germinate it to fruits. It is something that opens your eyes every morning and lures you to sleep every night. It is something that you will reciprocate to what your lover has invested in. And it is something fateful.

Love is a four letter word that, personally, I reckon people overuse it. There are different levels of love, like “like” and “adore,” but people always jump to love just like what mayflies do. Love is a gradual process, and since it is a product of fate, love takes time to form. And when fate strikes it away from you, it will still last. It will last forever, unharmed, unfazed, burning eternally and ethereally. And only fateful love is pink, because when your other half is gone, you still have your pinky pieces of memories to keep in your heart, instead of painting them the shades of grey. When you value love, you will respect every second of it. Hence, true love lasts, and it lasts by replaying the pinkness of fate, forever and always. Losing your true love will wrench you, destroy you, but it will make you a better person, a stronger man. Scared of love to the degree you want, but when you have the chance to grow it, don’t hesitate to nurture it against the odds of fate. It is the only way to overpower the challenges that the back sides of love eventually face you, to mark your love to be fateful.

Life will end, but when it is painstakingly fated, it will buzz.
