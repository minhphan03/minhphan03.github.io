Another year has passed and I’m about to turn 19. It becomes a cliche at this point to lament how fast time flies, doesn’t it? I wrote the exact same thing when I talked about how I was about to turn 18, and you know how I am totally not prepared to be 19. I mean, what’s the different between 18 years and 364 days old and 19 years old? Time is a social construct, people say. Maybe I should not hype my birthday up too much, it’s not going to do anything different anyway.

Hahahahahahaha no I will never stop, just because I did exactly that. I literally came home back from my birthday dinner with some friends and I am so full right now. My birthday is one of the very few days I can totally let go of my responsibilities and treat myself nicely for once. I can be selfish for a day, can’t I? That day is my birthday. I still love myself enough to not take away the only day I can feel good about my decisions (like buying boba without wincing at how expensive it is).

Like clockwork, I am going to talk about the changes and achievements I have done in the past year. I don’t think I have changed much besides finally experienced how crazy son of a bitch the US healthcare system is and how fucked up the job market is (and will be) *chuckles*. Right now I’m lying on my bed as I’m typing out these words, mulling over how I have to pay for everything I spend on my life now that I dont’t live in the dorms no more. My rent, my food, my aligners, my subscriptions, and everything I have to spend on if I don’t want to be a recluse. My parents help me with that partially, but do you get the idea that somehow you have to chip in as much as you can just because you are self-conscious about the money leaving your bank account? Just fucking great. 

But I have done some nice things this year. I finished my first year at UW with my overall GPA dropping not so marginally, and made some great friends I am still so proud to have. I think I had a crush on someone for the first time (and had my heart broken literally three days later), had my first internship (not a conventional one should I say), and rode a motorbike for the first time. I also passed the driving test and will go to the DMV office to get a drivers license (can’t wait for it). I made my first scrapbook, bought a new watch after 7 years, and went on a trip on my own in San Francisco last March. I opened my first credit card, bought a VIP ticket to a concert of a musician I like, and expanded my music taste. Maybe to some that’s not big achievements to have, but I think I did everything I could do in my situation. I will do some nice things on my 19th year on Earth too, and I cannot wait to embark on these journeys 🙂!

19 will be the last year I can proudly say I am a teenager. I don’t know what I will do if I turn 20 and step out of the teenage age. When Katy Perry sings “You make me feel like I’m living in a teenage dream,” I can totally feel the energy flowing through me. The youthful energy, the boundless, unfettered freedom and foolishness only a teenager can carry without any hesitation and frown from others. I wish I could still do that for more than just one year, because I don’t think I’m ready to let it go. I don’t go too far to say that my teenage years are literal “dreams” but I know that once I turn 20, things are going to change hectically. As far as social construct goes, 19 is a transitional year between children and adult, for some say that the human brain is not fully developed until one turns 25; however, I cannot afford to be naive and innocent in this world no more. When you’re 20, you’re basically U30 (lmao). Instead of enjoying my “teenage dream” I have to grow up to face the adult world. “It’s going to arrive like a hurricane, blowing us all out of the blue,” I wrote in last year’s entry — well it’s continue to hold some kind of truth then. The 19th year will go just like how quickly this year goes. Let’s just hope that it is going to be a good year. I don’t jinx it to be “my best year ever” because it will only disappoint in return, but how about some optimism? It’s not going to kill me, is it?
