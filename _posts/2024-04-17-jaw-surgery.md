Today marks six months from my jaw surgery (Oct 17th, 2023). My surgeon said the six month mark sufficiently determines whether a jaw surgery operation was successful as it is expected that all swelling disappears (numbing, on the other hand, may take up to three years). So, to celebrate this journey almost over, here is a collection of entries I wrote about this life-changing operation. This is the second major surgery I have, and the first one I was big enough to have a lasting memory on.

1. Day -1 (the day before surgery)

So finally this day has come: I will have my double jaw surgery tomorrow. My three-year-long anticipation and shame of my imperfect side profile has boiled down to this day. I have gone through a myriad of emotions to get to this: shame, hope, fear, longing, you name it. Surprisingly I don't have a lot of words to say about this, because I am too tired to process any outstanding feelings at the moment. I talked to my therapist earlier today about it and confessed to her how emotionally challenging this whole operation is to me, and she was really understanding. I guess it's a leap of faith I have to put into both myself and the surgeons to the other side... Deep down at the bottom of my heart I know that I have to do this sooner or later, and I am at the prime age to get this done and over with. Oh how restless I am right now. 

My parents sent me $20k for the surgery and I just paid off the hospital bills three days ago. Last week I went to Bartell's in my neighborhood picking up a hefty bag of medicine full of painkillers and antibiotics, which scared me to death. My cousin is reading a five page aftercare instructions for the operation. I just listed some of these details out to show you how serious this is going to be for me. To think about the fact this is my first major surgery, and I am doing this without anybody but my cousin is terrifying. I hope she can bear the mental stress of this, and how demanding this aftercare is going to be. If everything's fine, I will still be able to walk and not have to get bedridden for four weeks straight. Cross my fingers and pray for the best. Here's to a crazy jawline :)

Tomorrow I will have to wake up at 8am and take the bus to Kirkland, which is 1.5 hours away. At least my cousin is coming with me. I know she seems more nonchalant at things, and maybe that's a good thing. She takes everything with a grain of salt, and probably will take this hospital trip less serious than I will. I wish I would be at least as "stoic" as her, or maybe not have to worry as much on things I cannot change. This anxiety feeling is natural and expected. Just like the day I return to the US exactly a month ago, or before an interview. I guess this thing is on another level compared to the others because I spent so much time and money on this. $30k on the whole thing. Fuck American healthcare. Fuck insurance companies fucking up the medical industry.

3. Day 5: 
I’m getting there. I knowwww, it’s fucking cliche, but I’m getting there. Still numb as hell in my chin (IDK what the fuck they did with it but goddamn it takes so fucking longfor it to resolve itself), and tired as hell, but welp, it’s better than the other days. I couldn’t even get up to report the other days, that was how tired I was. Easily one of the worst periods of my life ever. I mean, mental pain is bad, but mental pain + endured physical pain?? Oh yeah get in loser.

I had this small uncomfortable point at the maxilla vomer/nasal conchea (I swear I only use these scientific terms because I don’t know how to describe exactly where I feel these effects) that whenever I try to put slight pressure on the skin from outside it felt like I could feel the screws and plates emerging under my touch. Like there was a gap or air bubbles somewhere inside this area that pushed back against the insertions and tried to reject them. 

![Maxillae](/assets/images/Maxillae.jpg)
<p style="text-align: center">The maxillae (source on image)</p>

My lower lip area is still crazy swollen and tingling. I thought of drinking pineapple juice to reduce swelling by buying a bunch of Dole’s juice from Costco, which a lot of people online said would work, but it felt like it didn’t really help. Somehow the inflammation got worse after drinking them sometimes due to the bromeclain in the juice increasing the blood flow and speed up all of these healing reactions (that swelling is one of them). I still think it’s quite tasty, but maybe I’ll stop drinking them for a while. A lot of people said that it is not that helpful, either, so we’ll see. I still have 30 cans, so yeahhh.

The worst thing about this new change is that I cannot risk myself standing up for long, because if I do, I’ll get nosebleeds. My head feels super heavy and rigid, and rotating my head sucks. This also means that I can’t leave the house, which I really want to as a Tuesday night movie plan is looking real good lately…

Additionally, my jaws are now banded elastically with rubber bands (they literally screwed my jaws and put hooks on them so I can wrap the bands over). It should not be a problem until I am supposed to change them daily. I spent at least half an hour everyday trying to hook them over the hooks in the two farthest corners of the jaw as I had to stretch my mouth corners to the max, tearing them like twice (it hurt like HELL) and watched myself in horror as blood covered my fingers. If it weren’t for my cousin, I could have had fainted in there at least twice.

4. Day 6:

Tingling a lottt. By the way, I finally figured out how to insert the bands so I went in today expected everything to be way easier but nooooo the middle band turned out to be the most painful, which was surprising because it was so easy the last time. I bled some while inserting it over the hook and I wanted to k-word myself so badly. Later today I went to my friend’s house today to watch a movie with her and talking did not seem to be so bad, so at least I can feel somewhat more confident towards my meetings this Thursday. My nose did not bleed even when I walked for like 15 minutes, so that’s another good thing. But then I got my lips super dry due to the weather so it bled a bit. I had to borrow her red lip palm because it got so painful every time I opened my mouth to talk. Imagine how horrified I was when I wiped my mouth after showering tonight… I literally thought my lips was torn to shreds. At least there’s no more excruciating pain or extended periods of aches anymore. I’ll take the tingles over them any day.

I seriously have no clue if my swelling goes down comparing to yesterday lol

5. Day 8:

I went to my first postop evaluation today. They told I was doing very well but I still had to stick to a liquid diet for another week. I wasnt shocked when they said so, because it’s an outlier for someone to move to soft chews that early. 

I looked at my bottom set of teeth in the mirror and noticed one of front ones was already crooked. It was barely a week since I stopped wearing invisaligns! Guess I have to wear retainers for the rest of my life, then…

7. Day 12: 

I went out with some folks today to a Taiwanese restaurant.. I ordered egg and tofu soup since I could not digest any meat, and it turned out I could not eat the tofu either. I struggled just opening my mouth to eat, mostly because of the rubber bands I deliberately left on, which my doctor insisted to. I then slurped the soup and left the tofu and egg, as well as some fried chicken one of them gave me, to be blended with my carrot congee I ate later that day. It was not so bad, honestly, but I got tired of congee so much that I would rather not eat it. My cousin made too much, and I hate to throw it all away.
